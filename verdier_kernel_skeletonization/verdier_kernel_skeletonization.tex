\begin{abstract}
For four anchored semistable models of $xy=t^4$, we prove a one-sided
kernel descent theorem with geometric rational unipotent nearby
coefficients and complete inverse-image supports.  The actual counit
$U_b\star T(P)\to P\star U_a$ is invertible on a specified generated
kernel source.  This avoids identifying the pullback of a coarse
diagonal with a fine diagonal, and makes proper product direct image
strongly compatible with convolution.  Graph and rectangular kernels
provide the finite geometric control needed to prove boundedness
and the counit's invertibility.  The resulting anchored localization
admits a Morita realization preserving convolution and units, with
actual geometric Verdier mates and coherent support base change,
localization boundaries and refinement comparisons.  The cellular
target is constructed independently from strata and links.  An open
stratum example distinguishes geometric kernel maps from the larger
Hochschild mapping objects in the Morita target; the realization is
not fully faithful.  A separate appendix proves a local
point-valued completed-root realization and its carrier pairing,
with separate ambient graph calculations and two geometric coarse
rectangular tests.  Another appendix proves an obstruction to
ordinary stalk/pullback gluing of distribution diagonals.  The exceptional
mate retains a noninvertible nodal dualizing complex.  A global completed
root kernel calculus is a separate research problem; the main theorem
uses only the nearby-enhanced source and is independent of that problem.
\end{abstract}

\section{One-sided descent and the main results}
The central comparison is a map of actual kernels, not only of their
coefficient actions.  For a refinement, let $U_a$ denote its selected
pullback graph and $T$ proper direct image on the product of supports.
We prove $U_b\star T(P)\simeq P\star U_a$.  The comparison is
one-sided: it does not identify a fibre-relation kernel with a fine
diagonal.  Its counit description is what makes the ensuing
convolution and Verdier comparisons compatible.


Let $R$ be a complete strictly henselian trait with algebraically closed
residue field $k$, uniformizer $t$, and $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}k$.
Fix the coordinates and compatible base roots.  For $xy=t^4$ fix
$B=\{2\}$ and the index
\[
 \mathcal J_B=\{B,\{1,2\},\{2,3\},\{1,2,3\}\}.
\]
Let $X_J$ be the actual integer-cut models of \cite{Refinement}, and
$u_{JI}:X_J\to X_I$ their proper contractions for $I\subset J$.
All cut inclusions in this paper allow equality.
The coarse exceptional curve $E_B$ is $\mathbf P^1_k$, stratified by
$U=\mathbf G_{m,k}$ and its endpoints $q_0,q_\infty$.
For a locally closed union $S$ of coarse strata put
$S_J=u_{JB}^{-1}(S)$, with its full scheme-theoretic inverse image.
Products of supports in this paper are over $k$, not over $R$.

The coefficient algebra $H_{S_J}$ is the restriction of the actual
geometric nearby image of the constant coefficient, with its algebra
structure retained.  Base Galois action is forgotten, not replaced by
inertia invariants.  The ambient module category is the
fibrewise-cartesian category $\mathcal A_{J|B}(S_J)$ of
\cite{Refinement}; attachments between distinct coarse strata remain
arbitrary.  The smaller unit-generated category $\mathcal C_J(S_J)$
is not substituted for this ambient category.

The geometric inputs from \cite{Refinement} are its branch-algebra
comparison, the algebra-unit comparison $H\to Ru_*H$ on complete
coarse supports, and its ``Comparison of the ambient gluing maps''
lemma.  The latter compares arbitrary coarse attachments under
module pullback; it does not assert kernel descent on products.
We use also the actual composition of its integer-cut contractions.
The new product comparison, convolution closure and their joint
Verdier compatibility are proved here.  Thus these earlier geometric
results are inputs, whereas preservation by a generated kernel and
its product descent are conclusions, not defining conditions.

\subsection{Framework and input contract}
In the main text preceding the appendices, categories are
$\Q$-linear stable infinity categories.  The appendices instead use
their specified $\Lambda_r=\mathbf Z/\ell^r$-linear and integral
categories; rationalization there occurs only after the stated adic
recovery.  In either setting ``full'' retains mapping spectra.  We use a category enriched in
$\operatorname{Cat}_\infty$, equivalently its enriched nerve as an
$(\infty,2)$-category.  Its objects are decorated supports $(J,S)$;
its Hom infinity categories are the bimodule-kernel categories of
Definition~\ref{def:kernel-source}.  Kernels are 1-morphisms and
kernel maps are 2-morphisms.  For each finite string the composition
functor is the multiple-product shriek direct image of the relative
multibar tensor.  Substitution is the Fubini map of these diagrams;
the empty string is $\Delta_*H$.  Thus the enriched composition data
are specified by the bar construction, not by an unspecified choice
of associators in a triangulated category.  Restrict to the full
generated Hom categories; closure is proved below.  We call this
locally stable enriched category $\mathbb K_!$.  The notation in the
main theorem denotes this category with its decorations.

The supported category $\mathbb K_*$ has objects $a^D$ and Hom
categories $\mathbb K_!(a,b)^{op}$, realized by the actual bounded
Verdier labels.  Its composition is the dual of shriek composition.
Here $\mathrm{co}$ takes the opposite of every Hom category, leaving
objects and 1-morphism directions fixed.  Both geometric duality and
cellular duality are enriched functors out of this $\mathrm{co}$
category.  The cellular target is the enriched category of the
specified algebras and right-target-perfect bimodules.

We invert refinement \emph{1-morphisms}.  Precisely, a localization
is an enriched functor $L:\mathbb K_!\to\mathbb L$ for which, for
every locally stable enriched category $\mathbb E$, precomposition
is an equivalence
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{Fun}_{\mathrm{ex,ps}}(\mathbb L,\mathbb E)
 \simeq
 \operatorname{Fun}_{\mathrm{ex,ps},\mathcal W_B}
                       (\mathbb K_!,\mathbb E).
 \label{eq:localization-definition}
\end{equation}
Here functors are exact on Hom categories, transformations are
pseudonatural (their naturality 2-cells are invertible), and
modifications and their higher maps are retained.  The right side
is the full subcategory of functors taking every named refinement
graph to an equivalence; it therefore inverts their saturation too.
No Verdier quotient of an individual Hom category is meant.
Theorem~\ref{thm:anchored-localization} constructs $\mathbb L$ as the
full anchor category, rather than assuming a localization theorem.
In this model the localized Hom category between the images of
$a,b$ is $\mathbb K_!(a_B,b_B)$, with its existing mapping spectra.

Derived Morita theory supplies the general relation between
functors and bimodules, and the interpretation of Hochschild
cohomology as endomorphisms of the identity functor; see
To\"en~\cite{ToenMorita}.  Integral-transform comparisons for
quasicoherent sheaves on perfect derived stacks provide related
geometric background~\cite{BFNTransforms}.  That quasicoherent
setting is different from our constructible nearby coefficient
categories and does not supply their kernel comparison.
The geometric contribution here is closure of the specified kernel
family, invertibility of the particular one-sided counit, and the
compatibility of its actual comparison diagrams.  Morita coordinates,
opposite-bimodule duality and enhanced assembly are general mechanisms,
not new general Morita or six-operation theorems.

The companion manuscripts in \cite{Local,Refinement} serve as fixed
inputs for the dependencies listed below.  The cited version dates
identify the specific source versions used; these manuscripts are not
bundled with this public release.  Their original theorem labels are
retained.  The following are the precise geometric inputs
from \cite{Refinement}, in its fixed geometric rational coefficient domain:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \texttt{lem:branch-algebras} and
\texttt{prop:unit-generation}: actual branch algebra maps and
unipotent generation, with their module-valued evaluation maps;
used in Proposition~\ref{thm:cellular-reconstruction} and the geometric
generators for Proposition~\ref{thm:cellular-morita};
\item \texttt{lem:cell-algebra-unit}: the actual equivalence
$H_{S_I}\to Ru_{JI,*}H_{S_J}$ for complete inverse images of locally
closed coarse supports, as an algebra map; used in the generated
kernel convolution formulas and Theorem~\ref{thm:one-sided-descent};
\item \texttt{thm:cell-reconstruction}: the intrinsic cartesian
category on a complete coarse-stratum fibre is generated by its
regular $H$-module; used in Proposition~\ref{thm:cellular-reconstruction};
\item \texttt{lem:fibre-cartesian-ambient}: the actual adjunction
$u_H^*\dashv Ru_*$ identifies the selected categories, with arbitrary
coarse attachments and full module mapping spectra; used in
Proposition~\ref{prop:coefficient-duality} and the selected coefficient
comparisons underlying Theorem~\ref{thm:one-sided-descent};
\item the fixed integer-cut chart contractions and their actual
compositions.  Their algebra maps come from the one nearby functor.
\end{enumerate}
No product-kernel descent or new convolution coherence is imported
from that paper.  External products, module bars and support
base-change comparisons are constructed using the scheme six-operation
enhancement of \cite{LZEnhanced,LZAdic}; this input is functorial on
correspondence diagrams, including the pasting cells and adjunction
mates, not just six triangulated functors.  All cited local results
are used only on the four fixed models and complete coarse supports.

\begin{theorem}[Generated closure, one-sided descent and realization]
\label{thm:main}
For the fixed data above and the source of
Definition~\ref{def:kernel-source}, the following hold.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textup{(A)}] \emph{Geometric control.}
The graph and rectangular families of
Section~\ref{sec:kernel-closure} control the generated convolution
source.  Every generated kernel and every selected action integrand
is bounded constructible, and its action preserves the specified
coefficient type.
\item[\textup{(B)}] \emph{One-sided descent.}
For $a=(J_a,S_a)$, $b=(J_b,S_b)$, $c=(J_c,S_c)$,
$I\subset J_a,J_b,J_c$, $P:a\to b$ and $L:b\to c$,
product direct image $T_I$ and the
actual refinement graph counit give equivalences
\[
 U_b\star_!T_I(P)\xrightarrow{\sim}P\star_!U_a,
 \qquad
 T_I(L)\star_!T_I(P)\xrightarrow{\sim}T_I(L\star_!P).
\]
Together with the actual unit they make $T_I$ strongly compatible
with composition.  At $I=B$ they identify the refinement-localized
generated source with its full generated source at the anchor.
\item[\textup{(C)}] \emph{Verdier-compatible Morita realization.}
There is an enhanced realization into the independently constructed
target of Definition~\ref{def:morita-target},
\[
 \Sk^{\ker,\min}_{!,B}:
 \mathbf K_{\log,k;B}^{\mathrm{dec},\min,\Psi}
       [\mathcal W_B^{-1}]
 \longrightarrow
 \mathbf K_{\mathrm{cell},k;B}^{\mathrm{dec},\mathrm{Mor},r\mathrm{perf}}.
\]
Writing $\Sk_!$ for this realization, its specified evaluation and
Fubini maps give
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\ \Sk_!(L\star_!P)\simeq
                   \Sk_!(L)\star\Sk_!(P)\ },\qquad
 \Sk_!(\Delta_*H_a)\simeq\mathsf B_S.
 \label{eq:main-strong-realization}
\end{equation}
For $P:a\to b$, $L:b\to c$, the right side in Morita coordinates
is $M_P\otimes_{\mathsf B_T}M_L$; this fixes the composition order.
There is also a star realization on the corresponding supported
source and an enhanced equivalence
\[
 D_{\mathrm{cell}}\circ(\Sk^{\ker,\min}_{!,B})^{\mathrm{co}}
 \simeq \Sk^{\ker,\min}_{*,B}\circ D_{\log,\Psi}.
\]
The composition and unit constraints are coherently compatible with
these actual Verdier mates, biduality, coarse support localization,
and the support base-change and proper refinement diagrams used
here, including the four-model diamond.
\end{enumerate}
Here $\mathrm{co}$ reverses kernel morphisms, not their endpoints;
$\mathcal W_B$ consists of the specified refinement graphs.  No
unrestricted base-change theorem or full faithfulness of this
realization is asserted.
\end{theorem}
Part (A) is proved in Theorem~\ref{thm:kernel-preservation}.
The central step of (B) is Theorem~\ref{thm:one-sided-descent};
Theorem~\ref{thm:anchored-localization} deduces localization from it.
Part (C) uses the geometric mate and Morita comparison, assembled
with descent in Proposition~\ref{thm:joint-kernel-diagram}.
The superscript $\Psi$ is essential; the subscript $\log$ records
the model's origin, not a proved original log/root kernel realization.

\section{Geometric coefficient types and kernel sources}
\label{sec:types}
We use the enhanced constructible adic scheme formalism of
\cite{LZEnhanced,LZAdic}, on the scheme domains used in
\cite{Refinement} and rationalized to $\Q$.  For an ambient category in
which arbitrary relative tensors can be formed, use
$\mathscr D(S)=\operatorname{Ind}(D_c^b(S,\Q))$, with the tensor,
inverse-image and shriek direct-image functors extended continuously
from constructible coefficients.  Their right adjoints are taken in
this ambient category.  On the bounded constructible domains used
below they agree with the actual geometric adjoints.  Module mapping
objects are mapping objects in this enhancement, not maps of underlying
cohomology modules.  Ind-completion is an ambient device; it does not
assert constructibility of a relative tensor over a nearby algebra.

\begin{lemma}[Bounded objects in the ambient completion]
\label{lem:bounded-ambient}
For a constructible algebra $A$, the full subcategory of
$A$-modules in $\mathscr D(S)$ with bounded constructible underlying
object has the actual enhanced module structures and mapping spectra.
The same holds for bimodules.  Scalar geometric right adjoints, when
their actual values are bounded constructible, agree with the right
adjoints in this completion.  Direct image of module sheaves has the
same agreement on this domain.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $\mathcal C=D_c^b(S,\Q)$.  The embedding
$\mathcal C\to\operatorname{Ind}(\mathcal C)$ is fully faithful and
symmetric monoidal.  The coherent data for an action on a bounded
object involve finite tensor words in bounded objects; they therefore
use the same mapping spectra in either enhancement.  More explicitly,
for bounded underlying $M,N$, the free-module bar resolution gives
\[
 \operatorname{Map}_{\operatorname{Mod}_A}(M,N)
 \simeq\operatorname{Tot}_{[n]\in\Delta}
     \operatorname{Map}_{\mathcal C}(A^{\otimes n}\otimes M,N).
\]
This is the same cosimplicial spectrum for actual module sheaves and
for the completion.  Its totalization is taken in spectra; no
commutation of a limit with a colimit is asserted.  Applying the same
argument to the tensor product of the two acting algebras, with the
appropriate opposite algebra, proves the bimodule assertion.

For a scalar adjunction, test the ambient right adjoint against every
$C\in\mathcal C$.  The geometric adjunction identifies these maps
with those into the actual bounded right-adjoint value.  These $C$
are compact generators of the completion, so they identify the two
values.  For a map with an algebra comparison $f^*A\to B$, test the
right adjoint to module inverse image against free modules
$A\otimes C$.  Their inverse images are $B\otimes f^*C$; the free
module adjunction and scalar geometric adjunction give the same
representing value, with its restricted $A$-action, as actual module
direct image.  Free modules on the $C$ generate the ambient module
category, which proves the assertion.  This does not claim that an
arbitrary bounded-underlying module is compact as a module, or that
an arbitrary relative tensor or relative internal Hom is bounded.
Those boundedness statements must be established separately below.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Module conventions.}
Coefficient objects are right $H_a$-modules; supported objects are
right $H_a^{op}$-modules, equivalently left $H_a$-modules.  A shriek
kernel is left $H_a$, right $H_b$.  Dualizing its two commuting
actions gives left $H_b$, right $H_a$; we then exchange their written
order, obtaining left $H_a^{op}$, right $H_b^{op}$.  This exchange
uses the symmetry of complexes with its Koszul signs.  It changes
algebra sides, not the geometric endpoints.  For $N$ supported,
$p_a^!N$ is a left $p_a^*H_a$-module: use
$p_a^*H_a\otimes p_a^!N\to p_a^!(H_a\otimes N)\to p_a^!N$.
Thus $R\mathcal Hom_{p_a^*H_a}(P,p_a^!N)$ takes Hom of left modules.
Precomposition with the right $H_b$-action on $P$ gives a left
$H_b$-action, written as a right $H_b^{op}$-action after pushforward.
All exchanges below use this same convention on mapping spectra.

\begin{definition}[Intrinsic coefficient category on one fibre]
\label{def:intrinsic-fibre}
For a complete coarse-stratum fibre $Z\subset S_J$, let
$\mathcal C_J(Z)$ be the full category of bounded constructible right
$H_Z$-modules with unipotent lisse cohomology on each fine open
stratum and perfect node restrictions.  Each individual internal
branch attaching map from a node module to its branch link must be
an equivalence of node modules.  This condition is imposed on each
branch separately, not on their direct sum.  Morphisms are the
actual module mapping spectra.  Exactness of restriction and links
and closure of perfect/unipotent objects under retracts make this a
stable idempotent-complete category.  On $U$ it is the unipotent
lisse category; on a coarse node it is $\Perf(A_q)$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Star and supported coefficient types]
\label{def:coefficient-types}
For a coarse support $S$, put $U_S=S\cap U$ and
$Z_S=S\cap\{q_0,q_\infty\}$.  The category $\mathcal A_B(S)$ is the
full category of $H_S$-modules with bounded constructible underlying
complex, unipotent lisse restriction to $U_S$, and restriction in
$\Perf(A_q)$ at each $q\in Z_S$, where $A_q=i_q^*H_S$.
No invertibility is required of its attaching maps.
On $S_J$, define $\mathcal A_{J|B}(S_J)$ by the same fine-stratum
conditions, requiring branchwise cartesian coefficients only within
the complete inverse image of each individual coarse stratum.
Thus on the fibre $Z_{q,J}$ all its internal branch attachment maps
are equivalences, whereas its attachments to the coarse open stratum
are unrestricted.  This is an intrinsic condition on actual module
sheaves, not a condition of lying in a prescribed essential image.

Use ordinary Verdier duality
\[
 D_SM=R\mathcal Hom_{\Q}(M,\omega_S),\qquad
 \omega_S=a_S^!\Q.
\]
The supported type $\mathcal A^D_{J|B}(S_J)$ is specified as follows.
Its objects are bounded constructible opposite-action modules $N$
whose exceptional restriction to each coarse-stratum fibre $Z$
belongs to $D_Z\mathcal C_J(Z)$.  On the coarse open stratum this
means the ordinary Verdier partner of the unipotent lisse category.
Here $\mathcal C_J(Z)$ is specified by the intrinsic internal branch
conditions just stated.  Empty supports have the zero category.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[The two coefficient types are Verdier partners]
\label{prop:coefficient-duality}
Ordinary geometric Verdier duality induces an exact anti-equivalence
\[
 D_{S_J}:\mathcal A_{J|B}(S_J)^{op}
               \simeq\mathcal A^D_{J|B}(S_J),
\]
with the actual evaluation biduality.  In particular no stalk-perfect
condition is substituted for an exceptional-restriction condition on
the supported side.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For the locally closed inclusion $e:Z\to S_J$, geometric duality gives
$e^!D_{S_J}M\simeq D_Ze^*M$.  This identifies exactly the specified
conditions on each coarse-stratum fibre.  Conversely
$e^*D_{S_J}N\simeq D_Ze^!N$ identifies the reverse conditions.
Verdier biduality on bounded constructible $\Q$-complexes gives the
inverse equivalences.  Applying duality to the module action produces
the opposite action; the biduality map is linear because evaluation
is balanced for that action.  The argument uses geometric duality,
not tensor-dualizability as an $H$-module or as a sheaf.
\end{proof}

The convention of \cite{Refinement} is
$\widetilde D_S=D_S(-1)[-2]$.  Thus $D_S=\widetilde D_S(1)[2]$.
These categories are stable under Tate twists and shifts, but this
translation must still be made on named support objects and pairings:
$W_S:=D_SH_S(-1)[-2]$, so $D_SH_S=W_S(1)[2]$.  Globally
$R\Gamma(S,D_SM)\simeq D_{\Q}R\Gamma_c(S,M)$.
The regular unit is $H_S$, not $D_SH_S$ or $W_S$.

\begin{definition}[Actual kernel categories and fixed generators]
\label{def:kernel-source}
An object is a decorated support $a=(J,S)$, with coefficient type
$\mathcal A_a=\mathcal A_{J|B}(S_J)$ and algebra $H_a$.
For $a,b$, put
\[
 \mathscr K_!(a,b)=
 {}_{p_a^*H_a}\operatorname{Bimod}_{p_b^*H_b}
                         (\mathscr D(S_J\times_k T_K)).
\]
Let $\mathscr K_!^b(a,b)$ be its full subcategory on kernels whose
underlying $\Q$-complex is bounded constructible.  It is not restricted
by a condition on the kernel's action.  All kernel mapping spectra,
including noninvertible morphisms, are retained.

Specify the following geometric generators.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The unit $\Delta_*H_a$ at every decorated support.
\item For a coarse open or closed inclusion $f:a\hookrightarrow b$
within one model, both graph kernels
$\Gamma_{f,!}H_a$ on $a\times b$ and
$\Gamma_{f}^{t}{}_{,!}H_a$ on $b\times a$.
Their actions are respectively $f_!$ and the module inverse image.
The coefficient maps are the actual restriction isomorphisms.
Also specify the diagonal images of
$j_!H_{U}\to H_b\to i_*H_Z\to j_!H_U[1]$ for each coarse
open/closed decomposition, with these actual arrows.
\item For each $J$ and each endpoint $q$, the two rectangular kernels
$H_{U_J}\boxtimes H_{Z_{q,J}}$ and
$H_{Z_{q,J}}\boxtimes H_{U_J}$.
\item For $u:a'\to a$ a refinement on a complete inverse-image
support, the transposed graph kernel
$\Gamma_u^{t}{}_*H_{a'}$ on $a\times a'$, with the left action
through $u^*H_a\to H_{a'}$.  These are the distinguished arrows
$\mathcal W_B$.  Their actions are the actual $u_H^*$.
\end{enumerate}
The proper graph in the reverse direction is available in the
ambient kernel category, but is not declared an inverse kernel or
added to this generating list.  Proper direct image is already the
inverse on the selected coefficient categories; inverting a refinement
in the eventual source is a separate localization step.

The generated shriek source $\mathscr K_!^{\mathrm{gen}}$ is the smallest
full stable idempotent-complete family of subcategories of
$\mathscr K_!$, containing this list and closed under Tate twists and
finite composable convolutions.  Equivalently take finite expressions
in these operations, evaluate them in the ambient categories, and
retain all actual maps between their evaluations.  No quotient by
equality of actions and no preservation test enters this definition.
\end{definition}
We denote this enhanced generated source, with its fixed geometric
decorations, by $\mathbf K_{\log,k;B}^{\mathrm{dec},\min,\Psi}$.
The supported source uses the actual Verdier labels specified below;
its product-duality functor is denoted $D_{\log,\Psi}$.


\begin{proposition}[Ambient composition and geometric generator actions]
\label{prop:ambient-kernel-types}
Ambient convolution and $\Delta_*H_a$ define associative unital
composition on these kernel categories with their enhanced cells.
The graph actions in Definition~\ref{def:kernel-source} are the named
geometric functors.  These assertions do not imply that
$\mathscr K_!^{\mathrm{gen}}\subset\mathscr K_!^b$, or that all its
actions preserve $\mathcal A_a$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Form the relative tensor by its two-sided bar realization.
Inverse image, tensor and shriek direct image preserve colimits in
the specified ambient model.  The ordinary geometric projection and
base-change maps therefore extend to this realization.  On a fourfold
product, either iterated convolution is the direct image of the same
three factors, relatively tensored over the two middle algebras.
Fubini and tensor associativity give its comparison.  For any number
of factors use the same multiple bar realization and the composite
projection; its substitution cells give the associativity coherences.
The diagonal projection formula and the regular-module unit give both
unit cells and their compatibilities.  This is the ambient enhanced
construction, not a boundedness argument for its relative tensors.

For the graph of $f$, restriction of the two projections is $(1,f)$;
the projection formula cancels the regular source algebra and the
remaining direct image is $f_!$.  For the transposed graph, the
projections restrict to $(f,1)$, giving
$H_a\otimes_{f^*H_b}f^*(-)$.  These calculations use the same units
and counits as the geometric functors, hence identify their action
on morphisms too.  Each listed generator has bounded constructible
underlying complex: its coefficient is an actual constructible nearby
restriction, and the specified maps are finite-type immersions or
proper graphs.  This verifies the generators, not their convolution
closure.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Bounded star labels and their variance]
\label{def:star-kernel-types}
The supported object corresponding to $a$ is $a^D$ with coefficient
category $\mathcal A_a^D$.  The bounded star kernel category from
$a^D$ to $b^D$ consists of geometric labels
$P^D=D_{S_J\times T_K}P$ for $P\in\mathscr K_!^b(a,b)$,
with their actual opposite-action module maps.  This is a full
subcategory of left $p_a^*H_a^{op}$, right $p_b^*H_b^{op}$ modules.
The generating star labels are the duals of the specified generators.
Biduality gives
\[
 (\mathscr K_!^b(a,b))^{op}\simeq
                    \mathscr K_*^b(a^D,b^D).
\]
It changes the two coefficient types, not the order of the endpoints.
If a shriek composite is bounded constructible, its star-labelled
composite is $D(L\star_!P)$.  This specifies the star composition
convention on its bounded domain.  Boundedness of every generated
composite is proved separately in Theorem~\ref{thm:kernel-preservation}.
\end{definition}
Indeed duality reverses the two commuting actions; interpreting them
as actions of the opposite algebras in the displayed order gives the
stated types.  Geometric biduality identifies the full module mapping
spectra and their reversed composition.  The star-labelled unit is
$D\Delta_*H_a$, not an assertion that $D H_a$ is the shriek unit.

The actual star action to verify is
\[
 N\longmapsto Rp_{b,*}R\mathcal Hom_{p_a^*H_a}
              (P,p_a^!N),\qquad N\in\mathcal A_a^D,
 \quad P=D(P^D).
\]
This expression is covariant in the label $P^D$, since its Hom is
contravariant in $P$.  It is defined in the ambient module categories.
Its selected bounded domain and geometric identification are proved
below, rather than inserted in the definition of an allowed kernel.
Theorem~\ref{thm:kernel-preservation} proves that the generated
source is bounded, and Theorem~\ref{thm:geometric-star-mate} identifies
this actual geometric operation on its selected coefficients.

\section{Geometric kernels and a first convolution calculation}
For coarse supports $S,T$ write $p_S,p_T$ for the projections of
$S\times_k T$.  A kernel has commuting left $p_S^*H_S$ and right
$p_T^*H_T$ actions; input modules are right modules.  Its
shriek action is the actual functor
\[
 \Phi_P^!(M)=Rp_{T,!}
       (p_S^*M\otimes^L_{p_S^*H_S}P).
\]
For kernels $P:S\to T$ and $L:T\to V$, the actual ambient convolution is
\[
 L\star_!P=Rp_{SV,!}
  (p_{ST}^*P\otimes^L_{p_T^*H_T}p_{TV}^*L).
\]
These expressions are initially formed in the ambient derived module
categories.  Bounded constructibility and preservation of the selected
coefficient categories require proofs for the prescribed generators.

Definition~\ref{def:kernel-source} fixes the generators and their
ambient closure.  In this section we start checking their actual
integral transforms; later preservation arguments must apply to
that specified collection rather than redefine it by the desired
conclusion.

\begin{proposition}[Ambient rectangular convolution]\label{prop:rectangle}
At the coarse model, put $P_{S,T}=H_S\boxtimes_{\Q}H_T$ for
$S,T\in\{U,q\}$, where $q$ is either endpoint.  The geometric
projection and compact-support base-change maps give
\[
 \Phi^!_{P_{S,T}}(M)\simeq R\Gamma_c(S,M)\otimes_{\Q}H_T
\]
for bounded constructible module coefficients $M$, and
\[
 P_{T,V}\star_!P_{S,T}
 \simeq R\Gamma_c(T,H_T)\otimes_{\Q}P_{S,V}.
\]
These are natural ambient comparisons, with the indicated outer
coefficient actions.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
In the action formula the first coefficient factor of the rectangle
is the free regular $H_S$-module.  The relative tensor is consequently
$M\boxtimes H_T$.  Compact-support base change for
$S\times T\to T$, followed by the projection formula, gives the first
map and identifies it as an equivalence.
On $S\times T\times V$, the convolution integrand is canonically
\[
 p_S^*H_S\otimes_{\Q}p_T^*H_T\otimes_{\Q}p_V^*H_V,
\]
because the two middle regular factors tensor over $p_T^*H_T$.
Compact-support base change and projection along the middle factor
give the second comparison.  These are maps in the same enhanced
six-operation formalism, hence natural in kernel and coefficient maps.
On these coarse strata $H_U=\mathbf Q_{\ell,U}$ by smooth specialization and
$H_q$ is the bounded finite-dimensional node cochain algebra.
Thus the displayed compact-support complexes are bounded and finite.
The tensor calculation cancels a regular algebra factor, not a
Verdier dual of an attached coefficient.
\end{proof}

Write $A_q=H_q$ as its actual node cochain algebra.  The two tests give
\[
 P_{q,U}\star_!P_{U,q}\simeq A_q\otimes_{\Q}P_{U,U},\qquad
 P_{U,q}\star_!P_{q,U}\simeq
 R\Gamma_c(\mathbf G_m,\Q)\otimes_{\Q}P_{q,q}.
\]
Here $H^0(A_q)=\Q$, $H^1(A_q)=\Q(-1)$, while
$H_c^1(\mathbf G_m,\Q)=\Q$ and
$H_c^2(\mathbf G_m,\Q)=\Q(-1)$; other degrees vanish.
We retain the actual complexes and maps, without choosing a splitting
or replacing them by Euler characteristics.  Its compatibility with the localization boundary is proved in
Proposition~\ref{prop:mate-operations}; actual fine-model product
descent is proved in Section~\ref{sec:product-descent}.

\section{A finite geometric family controlling all generated kernels}
\label{sec:kernel-closure}
For $W\subset S$ a locally closed union of coarse strata, write
$e^J_{W,S}:W_J\to S_J$ and put
\[
 E^J_S(W)=e^J_{W,S,!}H_{W_J}.
\]
These are actual coefficient modules, with their restriction-induced
$H_{S_J}$-actions.  Extension by zero for a locally closed immersion
uses its usual open/closed factorization.  All intersections below
are scheme-theoretic complete inverse images of coarse intersections.
In particular their maps are not arbitrary fine support inclusions.

For $J\subset K$, supports $S,T$, and $W\subset S\cap T$, let
\[
 \gamma_W:W_K\longrightarrow S_J\times T_K,
 \qquad z\longmapsto(e^J_{W,S}u_{KJ}(z),e^K_{W,T}(z)),
 \qquad G_{J,K}^{S,T}(W)=\gamma_{W,!}H_{W_K}.
\]
The source action uses the actual nearby algebra pullback map; the
target action is restriction.  Also set, for $W\subset S$, $Z\subset T$,
\[
 R_{J,K}^{S,T}(W,Z)=E^J_S(W)\boxtimes_{\Q}E^K_T(Z).
\]
Call these the graph and rectangular families.  They are defined
geometrically on products, independently of the actions they induce.
Empty supports give zero.  In formulas we suppress the outer support
superscripts when the endpoints specify them.

\begin{lemma}[Derived support intersection]
\label{lem:support-intersection}
Let $H$ be the nearby algebra on a support $X$, and $e:Z\to X$,
$f:V\to X$ allowed locally closed immersions.  For
$h:Z\cap V\to X$ there is a canonical bimodule equivalence
\[
 (e_!e^*H)\otimes_H^L(f_!f^*H)\simeq h_!h^*H.
\]
It includes empty, open, closed and mixed supports, and is natural
in their support base-change diagrams.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Set $Q_Z=e_!\mathbf Q_{\ell,Z}$ and $Q_V=f_!\mathbf Q_{\ell,V}$, regarding these as scalar
sheaves on $X$.  Scalar projection for immersions identifies
$e_!e^*H=H\otimes_{\Q} Q_Z$ as an $H$-bimodule.  The relative bar
therefore cancels a free regular factor and gives
$H\otimes_{\Q} Q_Z\otimes_{\Q} Q_V$.
The scalar map $Q_Z\otimes Q_V\to h_!\mathbf Q_{\ell,Z\cap V}$ is the
support base-change/projection map; on every geometric stalk it is
$\Q\otimes\Q\to\Q$ or $0\to0$, hence an equivalence.
Scalar coefficients form a field, so no hidden scalar Tor term is
present.  This proves the assertion with both regular actions.
Projection and base change for the open/closed factorizations give
the displayed map and its naturality.  The bar proof works in the
ambient completion by colimit extension of these scalar maps.
In particular this is a sheaf support calculation, not a claim
about derived intersections of coherent structure sheaves.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Four geometric convolution formulas]
\label{prop:four-convolutions}
For $J\subset K\subset L$, all well-typed instances of the following
are canonical equivalences of kernels with both coefficient actions:
\begin{align*}
 G_{K,L}(V)\star_!G_{J,K}(W)
    &\simeq G_{J,L}(W\cap V),\\
 R_{K,L}(V,Y)\star_!G_{J,K}(W)
    &\simeq R_{J,L}(W\cap V,Y),\\
 G_{K,L}(V)\star_!R_{J,K}(W,Z)
    &\simeq R_{J,L}(W,Z\cap V),\\
 R_{K,L}(V,Y)\star_!R_{J,K}(W,Z)
    &\simeq C_K(Z\cap V)\otimes_{\Q}R_{J,L}(W,Y),
 \qquad C_K(A)=R\Gamma_c(A_K,H_{A_K}).
\end{align*}
The scalar complex $C_K(A)$ is perfect over $\Q$.  These are the
base-change, projection and algebra-unit comparisons themselves,
not identifications chosen from equality of the corresponding actions.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
First, on one support the regular coefficient factors give
\[
 E_T^K(Z)\otimes_{H_{T_K}}^L E_T^K(V)
             \simeq E_T^K(Z\cap V).
\]
This is Lemma~\ref{lem:support-intersection}, applied with both
regular coefficient actions.  It does not use perfectness over a product algebra.
The same identity holds after pullback to a product of supports.
Across a refinement we use only the actual algebra map
$u^*H_{W_K}\to H_{W_L}$, not an assertion that it is an
isomorphism.  The regular cancellation there is
$H_{W_L}\otimes_{u^*H_{W_K}}u^*H_{W_K}=H_{W_L}$.
Each support base-change map below is the scalar shriek
Beck--Chevalley map, extended to the relative bar.  The source
and target actions commute with every face and degeneracy of that
bar; hence these comparisons, their inverses, and their Fubini
pasting cells are bimodule maps.

For the first formula the two graph supports on the threefold
product have fibre product $(W\cap V)_L$.  On that fibre product the
middle regular algebra is pulled back from level $K$, and its tensor
with $H_{(W\cap V)_L}$ cancels by the regular-module unit.  Composition
of the two monomial model maps gives exactly the stated graph.
Shriek base change on the graph squares and composition of direct
images construct the asserted kernel map.

For the second formula, after the same cancellation and intersection,
the remaining source factor is the direct image of
$H_{(W\cap V)_K}$ along
$(W\cap V)_K\to(W\cap V)_J$ and then its immersion into $S_J$.
This map between complete inverse-image supports is proper.  The
actual nearby algebra unit of \cite{Refinement} gives
\[
 H_{(W\cap V)_J}\xrightarrow{\sim}
 Ru_*H_{(W\cap V)_K}.
\]
Its inverse, with the specified algebra actions, supplies the required
comparison.  Here is its linearity as a specified map.  Put
$A=H_{(W\cap V)_J}$, $D=Ru_*H_{(W\cap V)_K}$ and let
$\eta:A\to D$ be that algebra unit.  Regard $D$ as an $A$-bimodule
through $\eta$, and let $r=\eta^{-1}$ in that enhanced bimodule
category.  Multiplicativity of $\eta$ and its inverse give the square
\begin{equation}
\begin{CD}
 A\otimes D @>{1\otimes r}>> A\otimes A\\
 @V{m_D}VV @VV{m_A}V\\
 D @>{r}>> A .
\end{CD}
\label{eq:rectangle-graph-linearity}
\end{equation}
Here $m_D$ is the action through $\eta$; the right-action square is
obtained in the same way.  These are squares in the enhancement:
an equivalence of algebra objects has its inverse there, rather
than an arbitrarily chosen inverse on cohomology.  Restriction of
the source algebra and extension by zero retain these squares.
External product with $E^L(Y)$ retains target linearity, since
$1\boxtimes m_Y$ commutes with $r\boxtimes1$ by tensor functoriality.
Thus both outer actions on the second formula are accounted for.
External product with $E^L(Y)$ then gives the second formula.  This is where a genuine proper coefficient comparison is
used; an ordinary graph pullback assertion would not suffice.

For the third formula the middle variable is fixed by the graph at
level $L$.  Its coefficient restriction is the module pullback of
$E^K(Z)$, intersected with $V_L$, hence $E^L(Z\cap V)$.
The source factor $E^J(W)$ remains unchanged.  For the fourth formula
the middle tensor is the first displayed intersection module.
Compact-support base change and projection integrate it to $C_K(Z\cap V)$.
All steps are maps of the actual sheaf modules on the threefold
product and respect the outer actions.

The coefficient $H_{A_K}$ is bounded constructible, the support is
separated of finite type over the algebraically closed field, and
coefficients are geometric rational $\ell$-adic coefficients.
Constructible compact-support finiteness in the specified six-operation
domain therefore makes $C_K(A)$ a bounded finite-dimensional complex.
Alternatively proper pushforward to $A_B$ identifies it with
$R\Gamma_c(A_B,H_{A_B})$.  No compact-support complex is replaced by
its Euler characteristic.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bounded kernels and preservation of the selected types]
\label{thm:kernel-preservation}
Every kernel in $\mathscr K_!^{\mathrm{gen}}(a,b)$ has bounded
constructible underlying complex.  Its actual integral transform is
an exact functor $\mathcal A_a\to\mathcal A_b$, natural in all actual
kernel maps.  Moreover, for $M\in\mathcal A_a$ the integrand
$p_a^*M\otimes_{p_a^*H_a}^LP$ is bounded constructible on the product.
These assertions apply to the fixed generating collection and its
whole finite convolution closure; they are not admissibility conditions.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each comparable pair $J\subset K$, let $\mathcal N(a,b)$ be the
full thick subcategory of the ambient kernel category generated by
all the graph and rectangular families above, allowing perfect
$\Q$-scalar factors.  For a noncomparable pair use the zero category.
Every specified generator belongs to this family: the unit and the
localization diagonal terms are $G_{J,J}(W)$, the immersion graphs
are its corresponding support-to-support versions, refinement graphs
are $G_{J,K}(S)$, and the given rectangles are instances of $R$.
Each family member is bounded constructible.  The four formulas and
perfectness of $C_K(A)$ show that $\mathcal N$ is closed under
convolution on its generators.  Since ambient convolution is exact
in each variable and preserves retracts, this extends first in one
variable and then the other to all of $\mathcal N$.
Thus the independently defined generated source is contained in
$\mathcal N$, and every one of its objects is bounded constructible.
There is no assertion of a uniform amplitude for all finite words.

For preservation compute the family actions before taking any thick
closure.  The graph action is
\[
 \Phi^!_{G_{J,K}(W)}(M)
  \simeq e^K_{W,T,!}\bigl(u_{KJ,W,H}^*e^{J,*}_{W,S}M\bigr),
\]
and the rectangular action is
\[
 \Phi^!_{R_{J,K}(W,Z)}(M)
  \simeq R\Gamma_c(W_J,e^{J,*}_{W,S}M)
                      \otimes_{\Q}E^K_T(Z).
\]
The graph calculation is projection along its actual graph;
the rectangular one is compact-support base change and cancellation
of the regular source factor.  Restriction to a coarse support union
preserves the intrinsic fibre conditions.  Actual module pullback
under refinement preserves them by the ambient-gluing theorem of
\cite{Refinement}.  Extension by zero across coarse strata preserves
them too: a whole coarse fibre is either retained or is assigned zero,
so no internal fibre attachment is cut apart.  Hence the graph action
lies in $\mathcal A_b$.  For the rectangle the scalar factor is
perfect over $\Q$, while $E^K_T(Z)$ belongs to $\mathcal A_b$ by
these same restriction and extension observations.  This proves its
preservation.  Exactness and retracts extend the assertion to
$\mathcal N$ because the selected coefficient categories are stable
and idempotent-complete.

For completeness the stronger integrand assertion is checked before
pushing forward.  For a graph it is the graph extension of
$u_{KJ,W,H}^*e^{J,*}_{W,S}M$, which has just been shown to be a
bounded selected coefficient.  For a rectangle it is
\[
 (e^J_{W,S,!}e^{J,*}_{W,S}M)\boxtimes_{\Q}E^K_T(Z),
\]
a bounded constructible external product.  Exactness of the integrand
functor in the kernel extends this to $\mathcal N$.  This explicitly
rules out an unbounded relative Tor integrand in the claimed domain.
All functors and comparison maps were defined in the ambient module
categories, so preservation restricts them on their full mapping
spectra; it does not select a quotient of kernel morphisms.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Why the direction of refinement matters]
The proof uses the generating direction of
Definition~\ref{def:kernel-source}: refinement acts by $u_H^*$.
It does not admit an arbitrary reverse graph before localization.
Over a contracted fibre the latter would involve the coefficient map
$A_q\to H_z$ in the other module direction; tensor-perfectness over
$A_q$ does not follow from finite-dimensionality over $\Q$.
The four-formula argument, rather than an unrestricted finite-Tor
claim, is the closure proof used here.
\end{remark}

\section{Cellular coefficients from strata and links}
\label{sec:cellular}
We now construct the cellular category without defining it as a
geometric endomorphism algebra.  All data in the construction are
stratum and punctured-branch data at the coarse model.
Put $A=R\Gamma(U,\Q)$ and, for each endpoint $q$, let $B_q$ be
the cochain algebra of the punctured strict neighbourhood on its
included branch.  Retain the actual algebra maps
\[
 A\xrightarrow{\kappa_q}B_q\xleftarrow{\lambda_q}A_q.
\]
These are equivalences by the branch-algebra calculation in
\cite{Refinement}, with the primitive Kummer class and the sign of
the chosen branch coordinate.  They are not replaced by chosen
identifications of their graded cohomology rings.
Regard $B_q$ as an $(A,A_q)$-bimodule through these maps and define
\[
 \mathcal L_q(N)=N\otimes_A^L B_q\quad(N\in\Perf(A)).
\]
It takes values in $\Perf(A_q)$ because both algebra maps are
equivalences.  This is a branch functor, not a condition on attachments.

\begin{definition}[The cellular gluing category]\label{def:cellular-gluing}
If $U\subset S$, define $\mathcal E_B(S)$ to be the stable
homotopy-coherent comma category of data
\[
 (N,(M_q),(\alpha_q)),\qquad
 N\in\Perf(A),\quad M_q\in\Perf(A_q),\quad
 \alpha_q:M_q\longrightarrow\mathcal L_q(N)\ (q\in Z_S).
\]
Precisely, it is the pullback of the arrow category of
$\prod_{q\in Z_S}\Perf(A_q)$ along the functor sending
$(N,(M_q))$ to $((M_q),(\mathcal L_q(N)))$.
If $S$ has only endpoints, set
$\mathcal E_B(S)=\prod_{q\in S}\Perf(A_q)$; for $S=\varnothing$
use the zero category.  These constructions are stable and
idempotent-complete: the arrow category and the displayed exact
pullback compute finite limits, colimits and retracts componentwise.
\end{definition}

For two triples $E,E'$, the full mapping spectrum is
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
 \operatorname{Map}_{\mathcal E}(E,E') &=
 \operatorname{fib}\left(
  \begin{aligned}
   &\operatorname{Map}_A(N,N')\oplus
     \prod_q\operatorname{Map}_{A_q}(M_q,M'_q)\\
   &\longrightarrow\prod_q\operatorname{Map}_{A_q}(M_q,\mathcal L_qN')
  \end{aligned}
 \right),\\
 (f,(g_q)) &\longmapsto
 (\mathcal L_q(f)\alpha_q-\alpha'_qg_q)_q.
\end{aligned}
 \label{eq:cellular-maps}
\end{equation}
This formula includes the homotopies in the attaching squares.
Deleting an endpoint deletes its data.  Restriction to the open
stratum selects $N$; restriction to an endpoint selects $M_q$.
The closed costalk in the reconstructed geometry is
$\operatorname{fib}(\alpha_q)$, not $M_q$.

\begin{lemma}[Module recollement and the selected generators]
\label{lem:module-recollement}
The open/closed gluing and mapping formulas used here hold in actual
right $H$-module sheaves.  On $U$, bounded complexes with unipotent
lisse cohomology form $\operatorname{thick}(\mathbf Q_{\ell,U})$.  On a complete
coarse-node fibre $v:Z\to q$, the functor
$F_q=H_Z\otimes_{A_q}(-)$ identifies $\Perf(A_q)$ with
$\mathcal C_J(Z)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actions on $Rj_*V$ and $i_*M$ use respectively the algebra units
$H\to Rj_*j^*H$ and $H\to i_*i^*H$.  Naturality of these units
makes $Rj_*V\to i_*i^*Rj_*V$ linear.  An attaching map
$M\to i^*Rj_*V$ is a map of $i^*H$-modules, so its direct image
is linear too.  Forgetting module actions creates limits: an action
on a limit is its compatible family of actions, including their
associativity coherences.  Thus the finite homotopy pullback of
these two linear maps is the sheaf pullback with that action.
The pair $(j^*,i^*)$ is conservative on modules, because it is so
on underlying sheaves and the forgetful functor detects equivalences.
This proves the gluing equivalence before taking Morita coordinates.

Mapping spectra in this pullback description are the homotopy fibre
of the difference of the two attaching compositions, giving
\eqref{eq:cellular-maps} after the branch identifications.
The linear triangle $j_!V\to Rj_*V\to i_*i^*Rj_*V$ pulled back
along $i_*M\to i_*i^*Rj_*V$ gives the localization triangle and
its actual connecting arrow.  These constructions use finite
limits and the actual bounded values of $Rj_*$ and $i_*$;
Lemma~\ref{lem:bounded-ambient} identifies them in the completion.
No commutation of an unbounded relative bar with $Rj_*$ or $i^!$
is asserted.  The left functors and their module projection/base
change are obtained from scalar projection on free modules and
bar realization; their bounded values here were separately checked.

A unipotent local system has a finite filtration with constant
successive quotients.  Each lies in $\operatorname{thick}(\mathbf Q_{\ell,U})$;
finite cohomological truncations give the assertion for bounded
complexes.  Conversely long exact sequences and retracts preserve
unipotent lisse cohomology.  This also specifies the enhanced open
category as a full subcategory, not only an abelian filtration class.

Finally the actual algebra unit $A_q\to Rv_*H_Z$ is invertible.
The adjunction unit on a free $A_q$-module is this map; projection,
finite cones and retracts prove it for every perfect module.
Hence $F_q$ is fully faithful there.  Its essential image is
$\operatorname{thick}(H_Z)$, which is the intrinsic fibre category
by \texttt{thm:cell-reconstruction} of \cite{Refinement}.
This proves essential surjectivity and the selected counit, rather
than inferring an equivalence of module categories from an algebra
comparison alone.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Enhanced geometric reconstruction]\label{thm:cellular-reconstruction}
There is a natural equivalence
\[
 \mathcal A_B(S)\simeq\mathcal E_B(S).
\]
It identifies full module mapping spectra, the actual open/closed
localization triangle and its connecting morphism.  The attachment
$\alpha_q$ is unrestricted.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Lemma~\ref{lem:module-recollement} identifies the selected sheaves
on $U$
with $\Perf(A)$ via $R\Gamma(U,-)$: the unit generates, and the
mapping comparison on it is its actual cochain algebra.  Let $V_N$
denote the inverse Morita realization.  The branch calculation gives
the actual evaluation equivalence
\[
 R\Gamma(U,V_N)\otimes_A B_q\xrightarrow{\sim}i_q^*Rj_*V_N
\]
as $A_q$-modules.  It is the restriction/evaluation map on the unit;
exactness and retracts prove it on all perfect $N$.  Thus it
identifies the independently specified $\mathcal L_q$ with the
geometric link, including its action.

Given a triple, form in actual $H_S$-modules
\[
 F=Rj_*V_N\mathop{\times}^{h}_{i_*L(V_N)}i_*M,
 \qquad L(V_N)=\prod_{q\in Z_S}i_q^*Rj_*V_N.
\]
The first $H_S$-action is induced by the open-adjunction algebra map,
the other two by its closed restrictions.  Forgetting module actions
creates this finite limit.  Its restrictions are $V_N$ and $M_q$.
The open coefficient and all its links are bounded constructible;
perfect $A_q$-modules are bounded finite-dimensional over $\Q$.
Consequently $Rj_*V_N$ and the finite pullback are bounded
constructible, and $F$ satisfies exactly Definition~\ref{def:coefficient-types}.
No condition of invertibility of $\alpha_q$ was used.

Conversely an actual module has the attachment obtained from
$F\to Rj_*j^*F$.  The natural map to the displayed pullback is an
equivalence after $j^*$ and $i^*$, hence an equivalence by
localization.  Recollement in modules gives exactly
\eqref{eq:cellular-maps}, with $A_q$-linear maps in the last term.
Thus these inverse constructions are enhanced equivalences, rather
than bijections on isomorphism classes.
The triangle $j_!V_N\to F\to i_*M$ is the geometric localization
triangle; its connecting morphism corresponds to $\alpha$ under
\[
 \operatorname{Map}_{H_S}(i_*M,j_!V_N[1])
       \simeq\operatorname{Map}_{A_{Z_S}}(M,L(V_N)).
\]
Here the identification is induced by the connecting arrow
$i_*L(V_N)\to j_!V_N[1]$ of
$j_!V_N\to Rj_*V_N\to i_*L(V_N)$.  Pulling this triangle back
along $i_*\alpha$ gives the triangle for $F$, so its boundary is
that connecting arrow composed with $i_*\alpha$.  This fixes the
sign and rotation convention without choosing an extension class
from an abstract isomorphism of groups.
Endpoint-only and empty supports give the asserted product and zero
categories directly.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Generators and the cellular Morita algebra]
\label{thm:cellular-morita}
Let $g_U=(A,0,0)$ and $g_q=(0,A_q,0)$ in the cellular category,
including just the strata present in $S$, and let $g_S$ be their
finite direct sum.  Then $\mathcal E_B(S)=\operatorname{thick}(g_S)$.
Its independently defined algebra $\mathsf B_S=\RHom_{\mathcal E}(g_S,g_S)$
satisfies
\[
 \mathcal E_B(S)\simeq\Perf(\mathsf B_S),\qquad
 \mathsf B_S\simeq\RHom_{H_S}(G_S,G_S),\qquad
 G_S=j_!\mathbf Q_{\ell,U}\oplus\bigoplus_{q\in Z_S}i_{q,*}A_q.
\]
Omit absent summands.  If $U\subset S$, the algebra has blocks
\[
 \mathsf B_S\simeq
 \begin{pmatrix}
 A&\displaystyle\bigoplus_{q\in Z_S}B_q[-1]\\
 0&\displaystyle\prod_{q\in Z_S}A_q
 \end{pmatrix},
\]
where rows index targets and columns index sources.  This denotes
the derived triangular algebra with the actual branch bimodule
actions and their composition, not its cohomology algebra.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Each triple has the componentwise cofiber sequence
$(N,0,0)\to(N,M,\alpha)\to(0,M,0)$.
The first and last terms are generated by $g_U$ and the $g_q$
respectively, since their coefficients are perfect modules over the
corresponding vertex algebras.  Hence $g_S$ generates; conversely
these generators and their thick closure satisfy the defining
conditions.  Formula~\eqref{eq:cellular-maps} gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
  \RHom(g_U,g_U)&=A,\quad \RHom(g_U,g_q)=0,\\
  \RHom(g_q,g_U)&=B_q[-1],\\
  \RHom(g_q,g_{q'})&=\begin{cases}A_q&q=q',\\0&q\ne q'.\end{cases}
 \end{aligned}
\]
The off-diagonal term is the fibre of $0\to B_q$.
Its left $A$ and right $A_q$ actions are the branch bimodule actions;
there are no two composable off-diagonal blocks.  This constructs
the displayed triangular algebra from the cellular data.
The enhanced reconstruction sends $g_U$ to $j_!\mathbf Q_{\ell,U}$ and $g_q$ to
$i_{q,*}A_q$, so it identifies algebras with their multiplication.
Finally the Yoneda comparison for $\RHom(g_S,-)$ is an equivalence
on $g_S$.  Exactness and retracts extend it in each variable to its
thick closure, proving full faithfulness.  Its image is the thick
closure of the regular module, namely $\Perf(\mathsf B_S)$.
The same proof works for endpoint-only supports.
\end{proof}

In particular the endpoint generator is $i_{q,*}A_q$, not
$i_{q,*}\Q$, and the total algebra is not $R\Gamma(S,H_S)$.
The latter describes the smaller unit-generated cartesian category;
it misses the independent endpoint summands and their attachments.

\begin{proposition}[Fine-model coefficient generators]
\label{prop:fine-generators}
For $u=u_{JB}:S_J\to S$, actual module pullback
$F_J=H_{S_J}\otimes_{u^*H_S}u^*(-)$ identifies
$\mathcal E_B(S)$ with $\mathcal A_{J|B}(S_J)$.
Its generator is
\[
 G_{J,S}=F_JG_S
   \simeq j_{J,!}\mathbf Q_{\ell,U_J}\oplus
                   \bigoplus_{q\in Z_S}i_{q,J,*}H_{Z_{q,J}},
\]
and its endomorphism algebra is canonically $\mathsf B_S$.
These coefficient comparisons respect the actual attachment boundary
and the four-model refinement diagram.  They make no statement about
kernel categories on products.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Use the ambient-gluing comparison of \cite{Refinement},
Lemma \texttt{lem:fibre-cartesian-ambient}.  Its hypotheses hold:
the models are integer cuts, the contraction is proper, the supports
are full inverse images, and the coefficients on each contracted
fibre satisfy the internal branchwise cartesian conditions.
Over $U$ it is the identity.  Over $q$ its left adjoint is
$F_q(M)=H_{Z_{q,J}}\otimes_{A_q}M$, and the actual algebra unit
$A_q\simeq Ru_{q,*}H_{Z_{q,J}}$ makes this an equivalence onto the
intrinsic fibre category by its geometric unit generation.
An arbitrary coarse attachment is sent to the actual mate
$F_qL(V)\to L'(V)$ after $F_q\alpha$; the comparison uses
$Ru_{q,*}L'(V)\simeq L(V)$ and does not assume $L'(V)$ itself
is cartesian on the fibre.  This is precisely the full mapping and
boundary comparison proved there.
The same base-change formulas give the displayed images of $j_!$
and $i_*$, hence the generator.  Full faithfulness identifies its
endomorphism algebra by the actual functor.  The nearby algebra maps,
pullback associativity and the composite-unit identity give the
coefficient refinement cells, as in that paper's anchored theorem.
None of these steps takes a product of kernel supports.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Dual cellular coefficients and the Morita target}
Construct $\mathcal E_B^D(S)=\mathcal E_B(S)^{op}$ directly from the
cellular category.  Equivalently it is the opposite comma diagram:
the vertex categories are $\Perf(A)^{op}$ and $\Perf(A_q)^{op}$,
and each attachment arrow is reversed with all its homotopies.
This construction precedes geometric realization and retains the
entire link bimodule, not just duals of vertex cohomology groups.
The label $g_S^D$ is the object corresponding to $g_S$ in this
opposite category.  The opposite of a stable category is stable,
with cofiber triangles reversed and rotated in the stable convention.

\begin{proposition}[Dual generators and their actual realization]
\label{thm:dual-cellular}
There are equivalences
\[
 \mathcal E_B^D(S)\simeq\mathcal A^D_{J|B}(S_J)
       \simeq\Perf(\mathsf B_S^{op}),\qquad
 \RHom(D_{S_J}G_{J,S},D_{S_J}G_{J,S})\simeq\mathsf B_S^{op}.
\]
They are induced by actual Verdier duality and carry the cellular
biduality to geometric biduality.  The dual generator is
$G_{J,S}^D=D_{S_J}G_{J,S}$, not an assumed coefficient dual.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The opposite cellular category is generated by $g_S^D$, since
opposites exchange finite fibers and cofibers and preserve retracts.
Its endomorphism algebra is $\mathsf B_S^{op}$ with its multiplication,
by reversal of composition.  Yoneda gives its stated Morita
presentation.  Compose the opposite of the proved geometric
reconstruction with Proposition~\ref{prop:coefficient-duality}; this
realizes precisely the supported category independently specified
there by exceptional restrictions.  It sends the generator to
$D_{S_J}G_{J,S}$ and identifies module mapping spectra by
\[
 \operatorname{Map}(D_{S_J}M,D_{S_J}N)
                  \simeq\operatorname{Map}(N,M).
\]
These maps are induced by the actual duality functor, so their
composition is the opposite composition and applying them twice is
the evaluation biduality.  At the coarse model the generator is
explicitly
\[
 G_S^D=Rj_*\mathbf Q_{\ell,U}(1)[2]\oplus
                   \bigoplus_{q\in Z_S}i_{q,*}D_{\Q}A_q.
\]
Its closed restrictions need not be those of the star generator.
The global pairing uses $R\Gamma_c$ on the primal side; no replacement
by $R\Gamma$ on the nonproper open stratum is made.
\end{proof}

At the coefficient level the cellular duality in Morita coordinates is
$X\mapsto\RHom_{\mathsf B_S}(X,\mathsf B_S)$.
Indeed the geometric mapping object
$\RHom(DG_S,DM)$ is $\RHom(M,G_S)$, and Yoneda identifies the latter
with the displayed relative Hom.  This establishes the coordinate
formula without identifying geometric Verdier duality with a scalar
dual of the Morita module.

\begin{definition}[The cellular Morita target]\label{def:morita-target}
Its objects are the independently constructed $\mathcal E_B(S)$ and
their dual types.  Its Hom category from an algebra $B$ to an algebra
$C$ is the full stable category of $(B,C)$-bimodules whose underlying
right $C$-module is perfect.  Composition is relative tensor in the
source-to-target order, and the unit is the regular bimodule.
Use the actual module mapping spectra, not their groupoids.
\end{definition}
This composition stays in the stated Hom category: for $M$ right
$C$-perfect and $L$ right $D$-perfect, the exact functor
$-\otimes_C L$ sends the thick closure of $C$ into the thick closure
of $L$, hence into $\Perf(D)$.  Units and tensor associativity give
the enhanced composition cells.  No bimodule-smoothness assumption
has entered.  This also defines the analogous Hom categories on the
opposite-algebra coefficient types.

The duality of exact functors between perfect-module categories
induces on these Hom categories the relative dual
\[
 M\longmapsto M^D=\RHom_C(M,C),
 \qquad (B,C)\longmapsto(B^{op},C^{op}).
\]
It is right $C^{op}$-perfect by right $C$-perfectness of $M$.
To see its composition law without an unjustified scalar-dual
formula, use the elementary Yoneda argument for exact functors:
a $\Q$-linear exact functor $\Perf(B)\to\Perf(C)$ is tensor with its value on
$B$, carrying the induced left action.  Evaluation gives this
comparison on the generator, and finite cones and retracts give it
everywhere, also on natural transformations.  Conjugating such a
functor by $X\mapsto\RHom_B(X,B)$ and its $C$ analogue gives the
bimodule $M^D$, with the displayed endpoint order.  Composition of
these conjugations and evaluation biduality give its coherent
composition and double-dual cells.  This constructs $D_{\mathrm{cell}}$
in the target.  Its identification with geometric kernel mates is
proved in Proposition~\ref{thm:morita-star}.

\begin{lemma}[Relative dual as an opposite bimodule functor]
\label{lem:morita-opposite-dual}
For ${}_BM_C$, first form $M^\vee=\RHom_C(M,C)$ with its
left $C$-action by output multiplication and right $B$-action by
precomposition.  Its use as ${}_{B^{op}}M^D_{C^{op}}$ means exchange
of these two actions via graded symmetry.  This defines a
contravariant functor on full bimodule mapping categories.  For
right-target-perfect $M,L$ there is a canonical equivalence
\begin{equation}
 \nu_{L,M}:M^D\otimes_{C^{op}}L^D
       \xrightarrow{\sim}(M\otimes_C L)^D .
 \label{eq:relative-dual-composition}
\end{equation}
It respects evaluation, composition, the regular units and biduality.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Before exchanging sides, the map is the tensor--Hom evaluation
\[
 \RHom_D(L,D)\otimes_C\RHom_C(M,C)
       \longrightarrow\RHom_D(M\otimes_C L,D).
\]
For degree-zero elements it sends $g\otimes f$ to
$(m\otimes l\mapsto g(f(m)l))$; the chain map uses the Koszul
symmetry of tensor factors.  Balancing proves linearity for both
outer actions.  It is the regular evaluation for $M=C$, and both
sides are exact and preserve retracts in perfect right $C$-modules.
It is consequently an equivalence for the stated $M$; this argument
is natural in the left $B$-action as well.  Exchange the sides to
obtain the displayed map.  Constructing it by tensor evaluation on
relative bars makes it natural on the entire mapping categories.
For three factors both maps evaluate $h(g(f(m)l)n)$ with the same
graded symmetry; the ambient associativity cells identify these
evaluations.  Units and double duals use the same evaluation.
Thus these are the constraints of the exact-functor conjugation
already described, with their specific maps now identified.
\end{proof}

For a generated geometric kernel its image is now unambiguously typed:
\[
 {}_{\mathsf B_S}M_P{}_{\mathsf B_T}
 =\RHom_{H_T}(G_T,\Phi_P^!G_S).
\]
Preservation and Proposition~\ref{thm:cellular-morita} make it
right-target-perfect.  The geometric evaluation/Fubini comparison
$M_P\otimes_{\mathsf B_T}M_L\to M_{L\star_!P}$ is proved separately
in Proposition~\ref{thm:kernel-morita}; it is not part of the definition
of the cellular target.

\section{Actual Verdier mates and the supported kernel action}
\label{sec:verdier-mates}
Theorem~\ref{thm:kernel-preservation} puts the whole generated shriek
source in the bounded domain of Definition~\ref{def:star-kernel-types}.
Consequently its geometric dual labels and all their actual maps
now form the corresponding supported source: composition is
$D(L\star_!P)$, with the cells obtained from the already constructed
ambient composition and actual biduality.  This defines its labels;
the next theorem identifies their action by a geometric formula.

\begin{theorem}[The geometric Hom--shriek-pullback mate]
\label{thm:geometric-star-mate}
For a generated kernel $P:a\to b$ and $N\in\mathcal A_a^D$, set
\[
 \Psi_{P^D}(N)=Rp_{b,*}R\mathcal Hom_{p_a^*H_a}
                         (P,p_a^!N).
\]
This is a bounded constructible object of $\mathcal A_b^D$ with its
contragredient target action, and the actual evaluation and trace maps
give a natural equivalence
\begin{equation}
 D_b\Phi_P^!(M)\xrightarrow{\sim}\Psi_{P^D}(D_aM)
 \quad(M\in\mathcal A_a).
 \label{eq:actual-geometric-mate}
\end{equation}
It is natural in coefficient and kernel mapping spectra.  No
coefficient-dualizability of an attached module is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write $X=S_a\times S_b$, $A=p_a^*H_a$, and
$K=p_a^*M\otimes_A^LP$.  The stronger integrand assertion in
Theorem~\ref{thm:kernel-preservation} says that $K$ is bounded
constructible.  Geometric support duality and tensor--Hom adjunction
therefore give the actual comparisons
\begin{align*}
 D_bRp_{b,!}K
 &\simeq Rp_{b,*}D_XK\\
 &\simeq Rp_{b,*}R\mathcal Hom_A(P,D_Xp_a^*M)\\
 &\simeq Rp_{b,*}R\mathcal Hom_A(P,p_a^!D_aM).
\end{align*}
In the middle step the right $A$-action of $p_a^*M$ becomes the left
$A$-action of its dual; the right target action on $P$ becomes the
opposite target action on Hom.  Tensor--Hom adjunction here is the
relative bar/Hom adjunction in module sheaves, using
Lemma~\ref{lem:bounded-ambient} for the bounded module data and the
geometric adjoints.  It does not replace
$P$ or $M$ by a tensor dual.  The last equivalence is the ordinary
Verdier exchange $D_Xp_a^*=p_a^!D_a$ on constructible coefficients.

More explicitly the Hom expression evaluates against $K$ to
$\omega_X$.  Push its two arguments by $p_{b,!}$ and $p_{b,*}$,
use the support projection pairing, and follow by
$Rp_{b,!}\omega_X\to\omega_b$.  This pairing is the adjoint of
the displayed duality map and specifies which comparison is used.
Thus the construction is the geometric trace mate, not an isomorphism
chosen between two dual objects.  Module linearity follows by moving
the target action between the evaluation factors.

By preservation $\Phi_P^!(M)$ lies in $\mathcal A_b$, so its Verdier
dual lies in $\mathcal A_b^D$.  Every $N$ in the source supported
type is canonically $D_aM$ with $M=D_aN$, by geometric biduality.
This proves boundedness and the asserted selected target for the
already defined Hom expression.  It also shows that the Hom integrand
is $D_XK$, hence bounded constructible.  All three comparisons are
natural adjunction maps in the enhancement, proving naturality in
all maps of kernels and coefficients.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Units, convolution and localization of the mates]
\label{prop:mate-operations}
The comparison \eqref{eq:actual-geometric-mate} identifies the star unit
with the identity and gives the composition comparison
\[
 \Psi_{D(L\star_!P)}\simeq\Psi_{L^D}\Psi_{P^D}.
\]
For a coarse open/closed decomposition it takes the actual localization
boundary to the boundary of
\[
 i_*i^!N\longrightarrow N\longrightarrow Rj_*j^*N
                         \longrightarrow i_*i^!N[1]
\]
on the supported types, including its compatibility with inserting
another generated kernel before or after the triangle.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For the unit the shriek action is the identity by its geometric graph
calculation.  The evaluation pairing in the preceding proof is then
the usual Verdier evaluation, giving the identity supported action
with its actual unit map.  For convolution use the common integrand
on the threefold product.  Adjunction identifies both proposed mate
maps with the pairing that evaluates the two kernel factors and then
uses the composite support trace.  Tensor associativity, Fubini and
transitivity of this trace identify these pairings before taking
adjoints.  Thus their adjoints agree as natural maps.  Equivalently,
under the specified geometric equivalence they are the composite
\[
 D_c\Phi_L^!D_bD_b\Phi_P^!D_a
          \simeq D_c\Phi_L^!\Phi_P^!D_a
          \simeq D_c\Phi_{L\star_!P}^!D_a,
\]
where the middle cancellation is geometric biduality.  The preceding
pairing calculation is what identifies this expression with the
actual Hom--shriek-pullback operations.

The diagonal localization kernels act by $j_!j^*$, identity and
$i_*i^*$.  Naturality of the same mate on their actual kernel arrows
and geometric Verdier exchange give $Rj_*j^*$, identity and $i_*i^!$
in reversed order.  Fix the boundary convention as follows: dualize
the original cofiber sequence as a fiber sequence and use its
canonical stable rotation.  If its boundary is $\delta:C\to A[1]$,
the reversed boundary is obtained by suspending
$D\delta:D(A[1])\simeq DA[-1]\to DC$ with the canonical dual-shift
identification.  In cochain models the internal-Hom differential is
$d(f)=d_{\mathrm{target}}f-(-1)^{|f|}f d_{\mathrm{source}}$; its
induced dual-shift identification is used in this rotation.  Thus the
differential and shift signs are retained, with no independently
chosen boundary sign.  It agrees
with the pullback description of the boundary in
Proposition~\ref{thm:cellular-reconstruction}.
Finally convolution is exact in each variable and the mate is a
natural map of enhanced functors.  Applying it to the arrow/cofiber
category, before or after such an exact convolution, yields the same
boundary comparison.  This proves the asserted mixed localization
compatibility, not just equality of its three object values.
\end{proof}

\begin{example}[Both rectangular convolutions and their Verdier mates]
\label{ex:rectangles-with-duals}
For a rectangle $P_{S,T}=H_S\boxtimes H_T$, its actual supported action is
\[
 \Psi_{P_{S,T}^D}(N)\simeq R\Gamma(S,N)\otimes_{\Q}D_TH_T.
\]
Indeed apply Theorem~\ref{thm:geometric-star-mate} to the already
computed rectangle action.  Its scalar compact-support complex is
perfect, and global Verdier duality gives
$D_{\Q}R\Gamma_c(S,D_SN)\simeq R\Gamma(S,N)$.
The resulting comparison is induced by the same evaluation, so it
retains the actions and trace.
For two rectangles the shriek middle factor is
$C_T=R\Gamma_c(T,H_T)$; the supported middle factor is
\[
 R\Gamma(T,D_TH_T)\simeq D_{\Q}C_T.
\]
For $U\to q\to U$ these factors are $A_q$ and $D_{\Q}A_q$.
For $q\to U\to q$ they are $R\Gamma_c(U,\Q)$ and
$R\Gamma(U,\Q(1)[2])$; the latter has cohomology $\Q(1)$ in
degree $-2$ and $\Q$ in degree $-1$.
These are actual compact-support and ordinary complexes, respectively.
The scalar dual in this calculation must not be confused with the
relative algebra dual of a Morita bimodule.
Proposition~\ref{prop:mate-operations} applies to the localization
triangle convolved with either of these rectangles, thus connects
these non-diagonal calculations to the actual boundary maps.
\end{example}

\section{Kernel realization in Morita coordinates}
\label{sec:kernel-morita}
For $a=(J,S)$ use $G_a=G_{J,S}$ from
Proposition~\ref{prop:fine-generators} and the specified equivalence
$\mathsf B_S\simeq\RHom(G_a,G_a)$.  This uses the independently
constructed cellular algebra as a coordinate algebra.  It does not
redefine the cellular category as the image of a kernel functor.
The passage from exact functors on these generator categories to
bimodule coordinates is the derived Morita mechanism of
\cite{ToenMorita}; below we specify its evaluation maps and verify
their compatibility with the actual geometric convolution.

\begin{proposition}[Unlocalized enhanced kernel realization]
\label{thm:kernel-morita}
The actual generated shriek source admits a $\Q$-linear enhanced
realization in the right-target-perfect cellular Morita target,
with kernel image
\[
 {}_{\mathsf B_S}M_P{}_{\mathsf B_T}
       =\RHom_{H_b}(G_b,\Phi_P^!G_a).
\]
It preserves actual kernel maps and has canonical equivalences
\begin{equation}
 M_P\otimes_{\mathsf B_T}^LM_L
       \xrightarrow{\sim}M_{L\star_!P},\qquad
 M_{\Delta_*H_a}\simeq\mathsf B_S.
 \label{eq:morita-convolution}
\end{equation}
These are its enhanced unit and composition constraints; they satisfy
the associativity and unit cells and retain localization boundaries.
No full faithfulness of the kernel-to-action functor is asserted.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{thm:kernel-preservation} makes each $\Phi_P^!$ an exact
functor on the selected categories.  Its value on $G_a$ belongs to
$\mathcal A_b$, so its Morita coordinate is right $\mathsf B_T$-perfect.
The source generator endomorphisms act through $\Phi_P^!$ and the
target ones by precomposition, giving the two commuting actions.
Applying the actual integral-transform functor to a kernel map and
then applying this mapping functor defines the image on the entire
kernel mapping category, including its higher simplices.

Here is the composition map.  For $X\in\mathcal A_b$, geometric
Morita evaluation gives
\[
 \RHom(G_b,X)\otimes_{\mathsf B_T}G_b\longrightarrow X.
\]
Apply $\Phi_L^!$ and map from $G_c$.  This constructs a natural map
\[
 \RHom(G_b,X)\otimes_{\mathsf B_T}
                     \RHom(G_c,\Phi_L^!G_b)
      \longrightarrow\RHom(G_c,\Phi_L^!X).
\]
It is an equivalence for $X=G_b$, with the regular-module unit map.
Both sides are exact in $X$ and preserve retracts, so geometric
generation proves it for all $X$ in the selected category.  With
$X=\Phi_P^!G_a$, compose it with the actual Fubini equivalence
$\Phi_L^!\Phi_P^!\simeq\Phi_{L\star_!P}^!$ to obtain
\eqref{eq:morita-convolution}.  Naturality makes it source-linear and
natural in both kernel variables.  The unit calculation is the
actual diagonal action on $G_a$, hence the regular bimodule, not a
supported dualizing module.

To specify coherence rather than choose objectwise associators,
first take the enhanced action of ambient kernels on their module
categories from Proposition~\ref{prop:ambient-kernel-types}; the
preservation theorem restricts it to the selected full categories.
The Morita coordinate equivalences identify their exact functor
categories with the right-target-perfect bimodule categories: the
comparison is evaluation on the generator, and the same finite-cell
Yoneda argument extends it on natural transformations and their
mapping spectra.  Transport the actual composition diagram through
these equivalences.  The evaluation construction just given is
precisely its composition map, so the resulting associativity and
unit cells belong to the displayed comparison.  They are not chosen
after checking objects.  Exactness sends the specified kernel
localization cofiber sequence, with its connecting arrow, to the
corresponding bimodule sequence.  This establishes the claimed
boundary compatibility too.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[The Morita coordinate of the geometric star mate]
\label{thm:morita-star}
On the supported source the geometric Hom--shriek-pullback action
has bimodule coordinate
\[
 M_{P^D}^{*}\simeq
       R\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathsf B_T}(M_P,\mathsf B_T)
\]
with left $\mathsf B_S^{op}$ and right $\mathsf B_T^{op}$ actions.
This comparison intertwines units, convolution, localization and
biduality with the cellular duality, before refinement localization.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Use the actual supported generators $D_aG_a,D_bG_b$.
Theorem~\ref{thm:geometric-star-mate} and geometric biduality give
\begin{align*}
 M_{P^D}^{*}
 &=\RHom(D_bG_b,\Psi_{P^D}(D_aG_a))\\
 &\simeq\RHom(D_bG_b,D_b\Phi_P^!G_a)\\
 &\simeq\RHom(\Phi_P^!G_a,G_b)\\
 &\simeq R\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathsf B_T}(M_P,\mathsf B_T).
\end{align*}
The third line is induced by the geometric duality functor on
mapping spectra; the last is the proved Morita equivalence on its
two arguments.  Thus the comparison preserves both opposite actions
and all kernel maps.  Right-target-perfectness gives the relative
algebra biduality, and these same maps identify it with geometric
evaluation biduality.  It is not a scalar-dual calculation.

The geometric unit and convolution comparisons are those of
Proposition~\ref{prop:mate-operations}; their Morita coordinates are
the evaluation comparisons of Proposition~\ref{thm:kernel-morita}.
Both are obtained by applying the same enhanced mapping functors
to the specified evaluations, so their unit and composition diagrams
commute.  The cellular duality on bimodules was constructed from
exact-functor conjugation; the displayed calculation identifies
that conjugation with the already established geometric mate.
Applying this identification to the functor categories of arrows and
cofiber sequences gives the localization comparison with its rotated
boundary.  Applying it twice gives the biduality diagram.  This
constructs the unlocalized shriek/star Morita compatibility on the
whole generated source.  Actual product descent and its mixed cells
are proved in Sections~\ref{sec:product-descent}--\ref{sec:joint-descent}.

Writing $\chi_P:M_{P^D}^*\to M_P^D$ for this comparison, its
composition square is
\begin{equation}
\begin{CD}
 M_{P^D}^*\otimes_{\mathsf B_T^{op}}M_{L^D}^*
          @>{\mu_*}>> M_{D(L\star P)}^*\\
 @V{\chi_P\otimes\chi_L}VV @VV{\chi_{L\star P}}V\\
 M_P^D\otimes_{\mathsf B_T^{op}}M_L^D
          @>{(\mu_!^D)^{-1}\nu_{L,P}}>> M_{L\star P}^D .
\end{CD}
\label{eq:geometric-morita-mate-square}
\end{equation}
To verify the square, evaluate its two adjoints on
$G_c$, $\Phi_L\Phi_PG_a$ and their Verdier partners.  The upper
route is geometric evaluation on the threefold product followed
by the support trace.  By the definition of $\mu_!$ as Morita
evaluation followed by geometric Fubini, the lower route is the
same pairing, with the two regular intermediate factors contracted
in the order of Lemma~\ref{lem:morita-opposite-dual}.  Associativity
of evaluation and the support projection formula identify the
maps before adjunction.  The calculation takes place on the
multibar diagrams and is natural in their maps, hence gives the
specified square on mapping categories, not only on objects.
\end{proof}

\section{Proper product descent of the generated kernels}
\label{sec:product-descent}
We use the actual kernel categories, not a comparison reconstructed
from equality of their actions.  In this section $I$ is any cut model
below both endpoints under consideration.  All supports remain full
inverse images of strata unions at the original anchor $B$; using
$I$ as an intermediate model does not enlarge the support set.
For $a=(J,S)$, $I\subset J$, write $a_I=(I,S)$,
$u_a:S_J\to S_I$, and use the following two ambient kernels:
\[
 U_a=\Gamma_{u_a}^{t}{}_*H_{S_J}:a_I\to a,
 \qquad V_a=\Gamma_{u_a,*}H_{S_J}:a\to a_I.
\]
Only $U_a$ belongs to the specified refinement generating list.
The second kernel is used in the ambient category to construct maps;
it is not declared to belong to the generated bounded convolution
source or to be an inverse there.

\begin{lemma}[Ambient graph adjunction]\label{lem:graph-adjunction}
These kernels have actual unit and counit maps
\[
 \eta_a:\Delta_*H_{S_I}\longrightarrow V_a\star_!U_a,
 \qquad \epsilon_a:U_a\star_!V_a\longrightarrow\Delta_*H_{S_J}
\]
with the enhanced adjunction triangle cells.  The unit is an
equivalence on the full inverse-image supports in this paper.
No assertion that the counit is an equivalence of ambient kernels
is made.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Graph base change and the regular-module tensor identify
$V_a\star_!U_a$ with $\Delta_*Ru_{a,*}H_{S_J}$; properness makes
its shriek and ordinary images equal.  The unit is the actual nearby
algebra unit $H_{S_I}\to Ru_{a,*}H_{S_J}$, already an equivalence
on these complete supports.  The opposite graph composite is supported
on $S_J\times_{S_I}S_J$.  Its coefficient is the relative tensor of
the two pulled-back $H_{S_J}$ factors over $H_{S_I}$, formed in the
ambient category.  Restricting to its diagonal and multiplying gives
its map to $\Delta_*H_{S_J}$ by the diagonal adjunction; this defines
$\epsilon_a$.  It need not have a bounded source.

To check the triangles at the kernel level, whisker these maps with
the graph kernels and use graph base change.  The two composites are
the unit/counit composites for $u_a^*\dashv Ru_{a,*}$ followed by the
regular-module multiplication and its unit.  Their triangle and unit
identities identify the composites with the identity maps on the graph
coefficients; pushing along the graph gives the kernel identities.
The same constructions on the relative bar diagrams give their
higher cells.  This verification does not infer a kernel identity
from a possibly nonfaithful action on selected coefficients.
Their actions are the actual adjunction
$u_{a,H}^*\dashv Ru_{a,*}$, with the specified algebra restriction.
\end{proof}

For $P:a\to b$ define actual product direct image, restricting the
outer actions along the nearby algebra maps, by
\[
 T_I(P)=R(u_a\times u_b)_*P.
\]
Graph base change identifies it, including maps, with
\begin{equation}
 T_I(P)\simeq V_b\star_!P\star_!U_a.
 \label{eq:product-sandwich}
\end{equation}
Indeed the first graph inserts the source proper pushforward variable,
and the last one the target proper pushforward variable; their
regular coefficient factors cancel.  Composition of proper direct
images is precisely product direct image.  This formula is valid in
the ambient categories; it does not require that $V_a$ itself be a
selected kernel.

\begin{lemma}[Direct image of the controlling kernel families]
\label{lem:product-generators}
For $I\subset J\subset K$ the actual comparison maps give
\[
 T_I G_{J,K}^{S,T}(W)\simeq G_{I,I}^{S,T}(W),\qquad
 T_I R_{J,K}^{S,T}(W,Z)\simeq R_{I,I}^{S,T}(W,Z).
\]
They retain the outer actions and, for localization kernels, their
actual arrows and boundary.  Product direct image therefore sends
the controlling thick family $\mathcal N$ into its version at $I$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
On a graph, composition of its immersion with $u_a\times u_b$
is the diagonal immersion of $W_I$ followed by $W_K\to W_I$.
Composition of shriek images and properness identify its image with
$\Delta_{W_I,!}Ru_*H_{W_K}$.  The inverse of the actual algebra unit
identifies this with $G_{I,I}(W)$.
On a rectangle, proper product base change and projection give
$Ru_{a,*}E_S^J(W)\boxtimes Ru_{b,*}E_T^K(Z)$.
Proper base change for each complete support square and the algebra
unit identify these with $E_S^I(W)$ and $E_T^I(Z)$.
These are module comparisons, not just comparisons of scalar cohomology.
For a localization triangle the three maps use the same support
squares and their units, so exact direct image compares the entire
cofiber sequence, including its boundary.  Exactness, perfect scalar
linearity and preservation of retracts then give the thick-family
assertion on objects and all its actual maps.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[One-sided kernel descent and strong composition]
\label{thm:one-sided-descent}
For every generated kernel $P:a\to b$, the actual counit gives an
equivalence of kernels from $a_I$ to $b$,
\begin{equation}
 \alpha_P:
 U_b\star_!T_I(P)
   =U_b\star_!V_b\star_!P\star_!U_a
   \xrightarrow{\epsilon_b\star 1}P\star_!U_a.
 \label{eq:one-sided-descent}
\end{equation}
Product direct image restricts to the generated kernel sources and
has invertible unit and composition maps
\begin{equation}
 \lambda_{L,P}:T_I(L)\star_!T_I(P)
          \longrightarrow T_I(L\star_!P).
 \label{eq:product-convolution}
\end{equation}
These maps are natural on full kernel mapping spectra and give an
enhanced unital composition-preserving functor to the source at $I$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
We first prove \eqref{eq:one-sided-descent} on the controlling family,
not by assuming descent of arbitrary kernels.  If $P=G_{J,K}(W)$,
its right side is $G_{I,K}(W)$ by the graph convolution formula;
its left side is the same graph by Lemma~\ref{lem:product-generators}.
Under these specified identifications the map on the graph coefficient
is the counit on
$H_{W_K}=u_{W,H}^*H_{W_I}$.
It is the inverse of the pulled-back algebra unit by the adjunction
triangle.  Thus it is the claimed equivalence, not an undetermined
endomorphism of that graph.
For $P=R_{J,K}(W,Z)$, the rectangle--graph calculation on the source
side uses the proper algebra unit and gives $R_{I,K}(W,Z)$.
On the target side the counit is the one on
$E_T^K(Z)=u_{b,H}^*E_T^I(Z)$, with its actual support base change.
The unit on $E_T^I(Z)$ is, by proper support base change, the
extension by zero of $H_{Z_I}\to Ru_{Z,*}H_{Z_K}$, hence is
invertible.  To identify the map itself, write
$F_b=u_{b,H}^*$, $R_b=Ru_{b,*}$, $E=E_T^I(Z)$ and
$C=E_S^I(W)$.  Proper support base change identifies
$F_bE=E_T^K(Z)$ and identifies the adjunction unit
$\theta_E:E\to R_bF_bE$ with the extension of the algebra unit on
$Z_I$.  Graph base change and regular-module cancellation give the
following square of kernels on $S_I\times T_K$:
\begin{equation}
\begin{CD}
 U_b\star T_I(P) @>{\alpha_P}>> P\star U_a\\
 @V{\beta_{\mathrm{left}}}V{\simeq}V
       @VV{\beta_{\mathrm{right}}}V\\
 C\boxtimes F_bR_bF_bE
       @>{1\boxtimes\epsilon_{F_bE}}>> C\boxtimes F_bE .
\end{CD}
\label{eq:rectangle-counit-square}
\end{equation}
The right vertical map is also an equivalence.  The vertical maps
use the same source algebra-unit inverse as
\eqref{eq:rectangle-graph-linearity}; on the target they use only
proper support base change.  Under these maps the top arrow, defined
by the graph counit, is the direct-image evaluation followed by the
$H_b$-action, exactly the definition of $\epsilon_{F_bE}$.
Naturality of base change with evaluation proves the square before
any counit is inverted.  Moreover the adjunction triangle is
\[
 \epsilon_{F_bE}\circ F_b(\theta_E)=1_{F_bE}.
\]
Since $\theta_E$ is the specified equivalence, the bottom arrow has
inverse $1\boxtimes F_b(\theta_E)$.  This proves invertibility of
this particular $\alpha_P$, not just isomorphism of its endpoints.
All maps are linear for the two outer actions.  This proves the
assertion on both families as an actual kernel map.  Both sides of \eqref{eq:one-sided-descent} are
exact in $P$ and preserve retracts, even though the ambient counit
need not itself be invertible.  Hence the assertion extends to
$\mathcal N$ and in particular to the whole generated source.

Using \eqref{eq:product-sandwich}, define \eqref{eq:product-convolution}
by inserting the middle counit:
\[
 V_c\star L\star U_b\star V_b\star P\star U_a
       \longrightarrow V_c\star L\star P\star U_a.
\]
It is $V_c\star L$ applied to the just proved $\alpha_P$, and is
therefore an equivalence.  The unit comparison is $\eta_a$ of
Lemma~\ref{lem:graph-adjunction}.  For three kernels the two
composition maps insert counits at the same two middle places.
The interchange law identifies their order of insertion, with its
cell in the ambient enhancement.  The adjunction triangles give the
unit cells.  Iterated insertion on relative bar diagrams supplies
all higher substitution cells.  This proves coherence for these
actual maps, rather than selecting abstract associators afterwards.

In particular the compatibility needed for localization is the
following specified homotopy, with ambient associators suppressed:
\begin{equation}
 \alpha_{L\star P}\circ(U_c\star\lambda_{L,P})
   =(L\star\alpha_P)\circ(\alpha_L\star T_I(P)).
 \label{eq:descent-pseudonaturality}
\end{equation}
Both sides start at $U_c\star T_I(L)\star T_I(P)$ and end at
$L\star P\star U_a$.  In the sandwich expressions they remove the
same $U_cV_c$ and $U_bV_b$ factors; the ambient interchange homotopy
identifies the two orders.  The unit identity is
\[
 \alpha_{\mathrm{id}_a}\circ(U_a\star\eta_a)=\mathrm{id}_{U_a},
\]
which is the graph adjunction triangle.  These are homotopies between
the specified kernel maps, natural on their entire mapping spectra,
not merely equalities of their induced coefficient actions.

It remains to check that the codomain is the specified generated
source, not merely $\mathcal N$.  On every original generator the
preceding direct-image calculation gives its corresponding generator
at $I$: inclusion graphs, units, localization terms and the two
rectangles; a refinement graph gives the identity kernel on its
support at $I$.  Exactness preserves finite cones, twists and
retracts, and the now proved invertible $\lambda$ preserves every
finite convolution word.  Since the generated subcategories are
full, images of their arbitrary kernel maps also belong to them.
This proves the claimed restriction without changing the generating
list or imposing a preservation condition in its definition.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[What has and has not descended]
The key equivalence is after source whiskering by $U_a$.
It does not identify a fine kernel with ordinary two-sided pullback
of its coarse image.  In particular
$(u\times u)^{-1}\Delta_S=S'\times_S S'$ still need not equal
$\Delta_{S'}$.  For the diagonal we used proper pushforward and
then a one-sided graph pullback.  No equivalence of unrestricted
product kernel categories has been proved or used.
\end{remark}

\begin{proposition}[The actual four-model diamond]
\label{prop:product-diamond}
The two product-direct-image paths from $\{1,2,3\}$ through
$\{1,2\}$ and through $\{2,3\}$ to $B=\{2\}$ have the same
coherent comparison with direct product pushforward to $B$.
This holds on kernels and their maps, and respects $\alpha$, $\lambda$
and all coarse support localization diagrams.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For a chain $I\subset J\subset K$, proper composition gives
\[
 R(u_{JI,S}\times u_{JI,T})_*\,
 R(u_{KJ,S}\times u_{KJ,T})_*
       \simeq R(u_{KI,S}\times u_{KI,T})_*.
\]
The restricted outer algebra actions agree because the actual nearby
pullback algebra maps compose, not merely because their cohomology
algebras are isomorphic.  On graph coefficients the comparison is
$H_{W_I}\to Ru_{JI,*}H_{W_J}\to Ru_{KI,*}H_{W_K}$,
which is the direct algebra unit.  On rectangles it is the external
product of the two such comparisons; proper product base change
and its naturality identify this with the displayed composition map.
On all kernels we use this actual natural transformation of proper
functors, so it is already defined on arbitrary maps and higher cells.

The graph units and counits for a composite are the composite
adjunction units and counits, by their explicit construction.
Whiskering these identities in \eqref{eq:one-sided-descent} and
\eqref{eq:product-convolution} proves compatibility with $\alpha$
and $\lambda$, including when a rectangle is convolved with a graph
or a localization kernel.  Applying the exact comparison to the
arrow and cofiber-sequence categories gives the boundary comparison.
The integer-cut chart maps in \cite{Refinement} identify the two
composites in the stated diamond as the same actual scheme map.
Composition in the fixed enhancement therefore gives both paths
one comparison with its direct image, with the associativity cells
for longer strings.  This is a comparison on actual products; the
single-support module equivalence alone was not used as its proof.
\end{proof}

\section{Verdier, refinement and localization in one diagram}
\label{sec:joint-descent}
\begin{proposition}[Actual actions and proper Verdier product comparison]
\label{prop:product-verdier}
Write $F_a=u_{a,H}^*$, $R_a=Ru_{a,*}$ on the selected coefficient
types.  The product comparison has actual action
\[
 \Phi_{T_I(P)}^!\simeq R_b\Phi_P^!F_a,
 \qquad F_b\Phi_{T_I(P)}^!\xrightarrow{\sim}\Phi_P^!F_a.
\]
The second map is the action of $\alpha_P$.  Define
$T_I^*:\mathbb K_*(a^D,b^D)\to\mathbb K_*((a_I)^D,(b_I)^D)$
on labels, module maps and higher simplices by actual
$R(u_a\times u_b)_*$, restricting both opposite algebra actions
along the nearby algebra units.  It has the proper-duality equivalence
\begin{equation}
 T_I^*(P^D)=R(u_a\times u_b)_*P^D
       \simeq D_{a_I\times b_I}\,T_I(P).
 \label{eq:proper-product-duality}
\end{equation}
These identifications agree with the actual Hom--shriek-pullback
star actions, including their evaluations and biduality.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The first action formula follows either by applying the actual graph
actions to \eqref{eq:product-sandwich}, or directly by proper product
base change and projection.  The former calculation identifies the
map as the very same composite of the units and counits, so the second
map is precisely the action of \eqref{eq:one-sided-descent}.
This also gives, using the selected counit $F_aR_a\simeq\mathrm{id}$,
$\Phi_{T_I(P)}^!R_a\simeq R_b\Phi_P^!$.

Put $\pi=u_a\times u_b$.  This product map is proper, and $P$ is
bounded constructible by the
kernel-preservation theorem.  Proper Verdier duality applies to it.
Its comparison is adjoint to the pairing
\[
 R\pi_*P\otimes R\pi_*D P
       \longrightarrow R\pi_*(P\otimes DP)
       \longrightarrow R\pi_*\omega_{a\times b}
       \xrightarrow{\operatorname{Tr}_{\pi}}\omega_{a_I\times b_I}.
\]
Moving either outer algebra action between the two evaluation
arguments proves its opposite-action linearity, so this is an
identification of kernel modules with their actual maps.
It defines $T_I^*$ by actual product direct image, independently of
using the displayed equivalence as a description of its value.

On coefficient types the supported transition is
$F_a^D=D_aF_aD_{a_I}$, with inverse
$\overline R_a=D_{a_I}R_aD_a:\mathcal A_a^D\to\mathcal A_{a_I}^D$.
Biduality and the selected identities $F_aR_a\simeq1$,
$R_aF_a\simeq1$ give $F_a^D\overline R_a\simeq1$ and
$\overline R_aF_a^D\simeq1$.  Proper duality identifies
$\overline R_a$ with actual $Ru_{a,*}$ on the opposite-action
supported modules; this is a separate restriction of the geometric
functor, not the primal functor with an unchanged domain.
The actual star formula was identified with this conjugation in
Theorem~\ref{thm:geometric-star-mate}.  Its evaluation pairing, after
product pushforward, is the pairing above followed by the target
projection trace.  Performing instead the projection first and then
$u_b$ gives the same pairing: both are evaluation followed by the
trace of the same composite proper-support map.  Projection base
change and trace transitivity identify these maps before taking
adjoints.  Thus \eqref{eq:proper-product-duality} and the supported
action comparison are the mates of the shriek product comparison,
not arbitrary equivalences with its abstract dual.  Applying this
argument twice and using evaluation gives the biduality square.

For clarity, write $\chi_{\pi,P}:R\pi_*DP\to D R\pi_*P$ for
this comparison.  If $\rho:X'\to X$ and $\pi:X\to Y$ are proper,
its refinement transitivity is the specified homotopy
\begin{equation}
 \chi_{\pi\rho,P}
   =\chi_{\pi,R\rho_*P}\circ R\pi_*\chi_{\rho,P},
 \label{eq:trace-transitivity}
\end{equation}
under the proper-composition identifications.  Both adjoint pairings
are evaluation followed by
$\operatorname{Tr}_{\pi}\circ R\pi_*\operatorname{Tr}_{\rho}
=\operatorname{Tr}_{\pi\rho}$.  This explicitly identifies the
Verdier comparison along the two refinement paths.  Inserting a
convolution uses the same identity for support traces, with $!$ on
nonproper projections, and the projection pairing; it does not
replace those projections by proper maps.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[The product, convolution and boundary comparison diagrams]
\label{prop:mixed-comparison-diagrams}
The proper product comparison, the convolution constraint $\lambda$
and their Verdier mates are comparisons of enhanced functors.  They
commute with the specified localization arrows and their connecting
maps, including along the four-model diamond.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Here are the geometric squares used in the comparison; their
Cartesian status matters.  For contractions $u_a,u_b,u_c$, put
$X=a\times b\times c$, $X_I=a_I\times b_I\times c_I$ and
$Y=a_I\times b\times c_I$.  With the middle projections denoted
$p,p_Y,p_I$, the square
\[
\begin{CD}
 X @>{s=u_a\times1\times u_c}>> Y\\
 @V{p}VV @VV{p_Y}V\\
 a\times c @>{v=u_a\times u_c}>> a_I\times c_I
\end{CD}
\]
is Cartesian, and $s,v$ are proper.  The actual shriek
Beck--Chevalley map is $v^*Rp_{Y,!}\to Rp_!s^*$.
Its projection-formula mates and the composite identity
$p_Ys=vp$ give the comparison
$Rp_{Y,!}Rs_*\simeq Rv_*Rp_!$.
The additional map $\rho=1\times u_b\times1:Y\to X_I$ is
proper; its square with $p_Y,p_I$ and the identity of $a_I\times c_I$
is \emph{not} asserted Cartesian.  Instead its contribution to
composition is the middle graph adjunction counit.

More explicitly its support before that counit is
$b\times_{b_I}b$, with diagonal
$\delta_b:b\to b\times_{b_I}b$.  Its two coefficient factors tensor
over the pulled-back coarse algebra.  Restriction along $\delta_b$
and coefficient multiplication are exactly $\epsilon_b:U_bV_b\to1$.
This supplies the non-Cartesian middle face.  Adjoin to the displayed
square the support square for $\delta_b$ and the projection squares
for the two kernel factors.  This describes the cube as a pasting
of Cartesian Beck--Chevalley faces and the one specified counit
face, rather than treating the whole middle face as base change.
After relative bar realization, its two shriek routes are
\[
 V_c L U_b V_b P U_a\xrightarrow{\,V_cL\epsilon_b P U_a\,}
 V_c L P U_a,
\]
with the exterior faces given by the displayed proper-product
comparison.  This is precisely $\lambda$, so this diagram identifies
the actual map on all kernel morphisms.

For its Verdier mate apply evaluation on the same support product.
On each Cartesian face use the mate of the specified
Beck--Chevalley map, not an independently chosen isomorphism.
On the middle face evaluation is balanced over the coarse algebra;
it therefore identifies the counit multiplication with its Hom
mate.  Finally trace on the two outer routes is identified by
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}_{p_Y}\,Rp_{Y,!}(\operatorname{Tr}_s)
  =\operatorname{Tr}_{p_Ys}
  =\operatorname{Tr}_{vp}
  =\operatorname{Tr}_v\,Rv_*(\operatorname{Tr}_p).
\]
These are maps from the appropriate pushforward of $\omega_X$;
$!$ is retained on $p,p_Y$, which need not be proper.  Projection
pairing and trace transitivity in the enhanced six-operation input
make this an identity of the specified natural transformations.
It is the product/convolution/Verdier mixed square.

For precision about the diamond, write $f_{ij}$ for its actual
product contractions and $c_{h,g}:Rh_*Rg_*\to R(hg)_*$ for the
composition cell of the fixed enhancement.  Both paths to the
anchor are compared to the direct functor by these $c$'s; the
comparison of paths is $c_{\rm right}^{-1}c_{\rm left}$, not a
claim that chosen cells are literally equal.  For three steps the
specified simplex is
\[
 c_{h,gf}\circ(Rh_*c_{g,f})
   =c_{hg,f}\circ(c_{h,g}Rf_*).
\]
The algebra units are images of the same composition simplex under
the nearby functor.  On graphs it acts on their actual algebra
unit, on rectangles on its external product, and on the middle
counit face on the composite adjunction.  The trace calculation
above identifies its dual simplex.  These are natural transformations
of the full ambient functors, so restriction to the generated thick
subcategories retains the simplices on all their maps.

Finally let $Q_0\to Q_1\to Q_2\xrightarrow{d}Q_0[1]$ be a
specified localization triangle.  Each natural comparison $\tau$
above between exact functors $F,G$ induces the square
\[
\begin{CD}
 FQ_2 @>{F(d)}>> FQ_0[1]\\
 @V{\tau_{Q_2}}VV @VV{\tau_{Q_0}[1]}V\\
 GQ_2 @>{G(d)}>> GQ_0[1].
\end{CD}
\]
It is obtained by applying the same natural transformation to the
cofiber diagram of $Q_0\to Q_1$.  Whiskering before or after that
arrow is exact; taking its Verdier mate reverses the triangle with
the fixed dual-shift convention.  This proves the boundary and
diamond compatibility for the actual connecting maps.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Assembly from specified enhanced adjunctions and mates]
\label{lem:coherent-assembly}
Work in a fixed enhanced kernel bicategory with exact composition
constructed on relative bar diagrams.  Suppose given graph
adjunctions $U_a\dashv V_a$ and their enhanced unit, counit and
triangle cells.  On full stable subcategories closed under the
specified operations suppose that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the graph units and the whiskered counits $\alpha_P$ are
invertible, and the proposed $T$, $\lambda$ and $\alpha$ are the
sandwich and counit-insertion maps, with
\eqref{eq:descent-pseudonaturality} and the graph unit identity;
\item the base-change and refinement comparisons are the actual
pasting comparisons, identifying the adjunctions for composite maps
with the composite adjunctions;
\item the bounded duality has evaluation biduality, and the proposed
star and proper-duality comparisons are the mates of the same
evaluations and support traces, with projection pairing and
transitive trace as in \eqref{eq:trace-transitivity}.
\end{enumerate}
Then these specified comparisons assemble into a coherent diagram
of composition, units, base change, refinement and duality on those
subcategories.  Exactness adds their localization cofiber diagrams.
Transport by enhanced coefficient Morita equivalences preserves
this diagram and its mixed cells.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Construct the diagram first in the ambient enhanced categories.
A string of composable kernels is evaluated by its relative bar
multisimplicial diagram.  Removing a middle $UV$ uses the given
counit; independent removals commute by enhanced interchange, and
inserting then removing a unit is its given triangle cell.  Thus
counit insertion defines the composition constraints and all their
substitutions.  The first hypothesis identifies the named maps
with these constraints and makes them invertible on the selected
subcategory.  Pasting and composite adjunctions identify their
base-change and refinement faces by the second hypothesis.

Taking mates is the operation of composing with the given units,
counits and evaluation on mapping categories.  It therefore carries
the already constructed simplices and their substitutions to mate
simplices.  The third hypothesis identifies the named geometric
star maps with those mates: on each mixed face both adjoint maps
are the same evaluation followed by the same composite trace.
Evaluation biduality supplies the double-dual cells.  Restriction to
full stable subcategories retains these diagrams, since all named
operations and values belong to them.  Passing to arrow and cofiber
categories, and then through enhanced Morita equivalences, gives the
last assertions.

This is a restriction and identification lemma, not a claim that
finitely many equalities in a homotopy category determine every
higher homotopy.  The enhanced adjunctions, pasting operations and
trace diagrams are part of its input.  The mathematical checks in
an application are invertibility and identification of the
\emph{specified geometric maps} with these enhanced constructions.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Joint compatibility on the generated kernel diagram]
\label{thm:joint-kernel-diagram}
Product descent, kernel convolution, their units, coarse localization
boundaries, geometric Verdier duality and biduality form a coherent
diagram on the generated sources.  The Morita realizations carry this
diagram to the corresponding cellular one.  In particular the new
kernel operations and the actual product diamond, rather than only
the earlier coefficient operation diagram, are included.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
We apply Lemma~\ref{lem:coherent-assembly} to the actual
constructions, not to abstract objectwise equivalences.  Its first
hypothesis is Lemma~\ref{lem:graph-adjunction} and
Theorem~\ref{thm:one-sided-descent}, with the rectangle identification
\eqref{eq:rectangle-counit-square}.  Its second is the specified composition simplex and proper support
base-change maps in Proposition~\ref{prop:mixed-comparison-diagrams},
together with Proposition~\ref{prop:product-diamond}.  Its third is
Theorem~\ref{thm:geometric-star-mate},
Proposition~\ref{prop:mate-operations} and
Proposition~\ref{prop:product-verdier}, with the mixed cube and
trace identity of Proposition~\ref{prop:mixed-comparison-diagrams}.
Boundedness and closure are
Theorem~\ref{thm:kernel-preservation}.  We spell out the mixed
geometric identification and its Morita image below.

First work in the fixed ambient enhanced kernel and module categories.
All kernel arrows are retained.  The shriek part of the diagram is
built from the specified convolution functors, proper product direct
images, $\eta$, $\epsilon$, and their whiskerings.  The maps
$\alpha$ and $\lambda$ are those whiskerings, not independent
choices.  Their substitution identities are the interchange and
adjunction identities verified in Theorem~\ref{thm:one-sided-descent}.
Composition of proper images with these maps is the composite
adjunction identity of Proposition~\ref{prop:product-diamond}.
Those arguments also identify the elementary geometric maps with
these enhanced cells.

Include the dual types and evaluations.  Verdier duality here is
applied to the bounded kernels and whiskered comparison maps proved
above, not to an asserted reflexive structure on the possibly
unbounded ambient $U_a\star V_a$.  The mate theorem identifies
each star action with its actual Hom--shriek-pullback expression.
Proposition~\ref{prop:product-verdier} identifies its new proper
comparison with the trace mate.  For a composite of a convolution
square and a product square, both proposed Verdier comparisons are
adjoint to evaluation on the same multiple product followed by the
same composite trace.  Interchange, the projection map and trace
transitivity identify these evaluations.  This proves the mixed
Verdier/convolution/refinement square, not just its separate edges.
The same evaluation supplies the unit and biduality squares.
The functors in question are exact, with duality contravariantly
exact.  Apply these specified maps to their full arrow and cofiber
categories; their naturality identifies the mixed localization
boundary with the dual-shift convention fixed above.

These constructions are made on the relative bar diagrams and the
functor categories of strings of actual maps.  Whiskering, taking
mates and taking cofibers are operations on these enhanced categories,
so they send the entire simplices and their substitutions to the
corresponding simplices.  The unit, interchange and trace identities
just checked identify the required geometric generators of this
operation diagram.  Restricting to the proved bounded generated
subcategories therefore retains all its higher mixed cells.  We do
not claim uniqueness of an arbitrary enhancement of every six
operation, nor invoke the old coefficient transport theorem for
unverified new operations.

Finally, in generator coordinates the action formula and the actual
adjunction $F_b\dashv R_b$ give
\[
 M_{T_I(P)}
 =\RHom(G_{b_I},R_b\Phi_P^!F_aG_{a_I})
 \simeq\RHom(F_bG_{b_I},\Phi_P^!G_a)=M_P.
\]
Here $F_aG_{a_I}=G_a$ and $F_bG_{b_I}=G_b$ are the already specified
geometric generator comparisons.  This equivalence is natural on
kernel maps and respects both source and target actions.  Denote
it by $\sigma_P$, and denote the evaluation constraint
\eqref{eq:morita-convolution} by $\mu_{L,P}$.  The compatibility that
allows strong convolution to descend is explicitly
\begin{equation}
 \sigma_{L\star P}\circ M(\lambda_{L,P})\circ
                 \mu_{T_I(L),T_I(P)}
   =\mu_{L,P}\circ(\sigma_P\otimes\sigma_L).
 \label{eq:morita-descent-square}
\end{equation}
Both sides map $M_{T_I(P)}\otimes M_{T_I(L)}$ to $M_{L\star P}$,
using the specified identifications of endpoint algebras.
Applying
the same adjunction to the evaluation map defining Morita composition
identifies the two tensor comparisons; its triangle identities give
the unit comparison.  On the dual generators the proper evaluation
just checked gives the relative algebra dual comparison of
Proposition~\ref{thm:morita-star}.  Hence it also retains biduality and
the boundary maps.  The full exact-functor/Morita equivalences
transport the entire diagram and its higher cells.  This last
transport is applied only after the new kernel and product maps
have been constructed and identified geometrically.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Anchored localization]\label{thm:anchored-localization}
The two enhanced Morita realizations descend through the specified
refinement localizations and satisfy the equivalence in the main
theorem, preserving units, convolution, localization, biduality and
the actual four-model comparison.  More precisely product direct image
to $B$ induces an equivalence between the localized generated shriek
source and its full subcategory of objects at $B$, with the generated
kernels there.  The dual statement holds for the supported source.
This is anchored independence for these sources, not for all
geometric kernels or for a change of anchor.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $T_B$ be the enhanced product functor just constructed, and let
$\iota$ include the kernel source at $B$.  The restricted composite
$T_B\iota$ is the identity, with the identity proper maps and their
unit cells.  Every distinguished refinement graph is sent by $T_B$
to the regular unit kernel on its coarse support.  Thus $T_B$ factors
through the enhanced localization at $\mathcal W_B$.

The components $U_a:\iota T_B(a)\to a$, together with the invertible
kernel maps $\alpha_P$, give a coherent natural comparison from
$\iota T_B$ to the identity on the generated source.  Its compatibility
with composition is precisely
\eqref{eq:descent-pseudonaturality}, and its unit compatibility is the
graph triangle identity proved there.
These components become equivalences after inverting
$\mathcal W_B$; for composite refinements the same conclusion follows
from their graph composition.  Hence $\iota$ and the descended $T_B$
are inverse equivalences there.  Equivalently these identities verify
the localization universal property directly: any enhanced functor
inverting the $U_a$ is identified, by its images of the $\alpha_P$,
with its restriction to the anchor composed with $T_B$.  The same
comparison on functor mapping categories retains transformations and
their higher cells.  More explicitly, precomposition with $T_B$ and
$\iota$ gives functors in the two directions of
\eqref{eq:localization-definition}.  Their composite on the anchor
side is the identity.  On a functor $F$ inverting $\mathcal W_B$,
the other composite has comparison with $F$ given by $F(U_a)$
and $F(\alpha_P)$.  For a pseudonatural transformation $\tau:F\to F'$
its naturality square at $U_a$ is exactly the modification relating
these comparisons.  Naturality on modifications gives the same
construction on their mapping spaces.  Since $F(U_a)$ are
equivalences, this is an equivalence of these functor categories,
proving the stipulated universal property with the anchor as the
explicit localization model.  This argument uses the actual maps
$\alpha_P$ between generated kernels; it neither quotients kernel
maps by equality of action nor inserts a nonselected reverse graph
as an a priori inverse.

The shriek Morita functor sends $U_a$ to the bimodule representing
$F_a$, already an equivalence of the selected coefficient categories.
It therefore descends by the universal property of localization.
The supported functor does the same for the dual graph arrows, which
act by the proved geometric $F_a^D$.  The geometric and cellular
dualities carry the distinguished arrows to these supported arrows.
The enhanced natural equivalence proved before localization, with
its entire compatible diagram in Proposition~\ref{thm:joint-kernel-diagram},
therefore descends as well.  This proves the main theorem.
All product and trace comparisons remain those of the fixed maps;
the localization is performed only after their verification.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[A noninvertible kernel survives localization]
\label{prop:noninvertible-rectangle}
The coarse rectangular kernel $P_{Uq}=H_U\boxtimes A_q$, for either
endpoint $q$, is not invertible in the anchored localized source.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Its actual action sends $\mathbf Q_{\ell,U}$ to $C\otimes A_q$, where
$C=R\Gamma_c(U,\Q)$.  The source endomorphism complex is
$A=R\Gamma(U,\Q)$, whose total cohomology dimension is two.  Since
$C$ is a perfect $\Q$-complex, the target endomorphism complex is
\[
 \RHom_{A_q}(C\otimes A_q,C\otimes A_q)
   \simeq\RHom_{\Q}(C,C)\otimes A_q.
\]
The cohomology of $C$ is one-dimensional in each of degrees $1,2$,
and that of $A_q$ is one-dimensional in degrees $0,1$.  Kunneth
therefore gives total dimension eight for this endomorphism complex
(in degrees $-1,0,1,2$ the dimensions are $1,3,3,1$).
Thus the action is not fully faithful and cannot be an equivalence.
The Morita realization factors through refinement localization by
Theorem~\ref{thm:anchored-localization}.  An invertible kernel there
would have an invertible Morita image and hence an equivalence as
its coefficient action, contradicting this calculation.  No faithfulness
of the kernel realization is used in this implication.
\end{proof}

The following example uses the standard Hochschild interpretation
of identity-functor endomorphisms~\cite{ToenMorita}.  Its geometric
source consists of constructible $\ell$-adic kernels acting on the
selected coefficient category.  It is not the quasicoherent kernel
category in the integral-transform comparisons of
\cite{BFNTransforms}.  Full bimodule mapping objects in the Morita
target can therefore contain maps absent from this geometric source;
we compute that difference explicitly rather than importing a
quasicoherent kernel--functor equivalence.

\begin{proposition}[Geometric unit maps and Hochschild mapping-object discrepancy]
\label{prop:hochschild-boundary}
On the coarse open stratum $U=\mathbf G_m$, put
$A=R\Gamma(U,\Q)$ and $I_U=\Delta_*\mathbf Q_{\ell,U}$.  The geometric source
and its Morita target have mapping objects
\[
 \RHom_{U\times U}(I_U,I_U)\simeq A,
 \qquad
 \RHom_{A^e}(\Sk_!(I_U),\Sk_!(I_U))
       \simeq\RHom_{A^e}(A,A).
\]
Their degree-zero cohomology groups are not isomorphic.  With the
geometric coefficients of this paper, a choice of generator of
$H^1(U,\Q)$ identifies the second with $\Q[[z]]$, whereas the
first is $\Q$.  The realization, also after anchored localization,
is consequently not fully faithful on kernel mapping objects.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Here $H_U=\mathbf Q_{\ell,U}$, so the geometric module-kernel mapping object is
ordinary sheaf Hom.  Closed-immersion direct image along $\Delta$
is fully faithful, with $\Delta^*\Delta_*\simeq1$.  Its adjunction
gives the first equivalence.  The unit calculation in
Proposition~\ref{thm:kernel-morita} gives the regular bimodule $A$,
so the target, which retains full bimodule mapping spectra, gives
the second.  Right-target-perfectness does not require $A$ to be
perfect over $A^e$ and does not truncate this Hom.

For completeness, compute the latter Hom rather than replacing its
totalization by a direct sum.  Over the geometric field, choose a
basis of the one-dimensional Tate line $H^1(U,\Q)=\Q(-1)$.
We use characteristic-zero rectification here: commutative dg
$\Q$-algebras model $E_\infty$ algebras in complexes, since the
finite symmetric-group coinvariants are exact over $\Q$ and the
commutative operad is equivalent to its $E_\infty$ resolution.
Derived global sections of the constant commutative algebra supply
such an $E_\infty$ model.  A rectified commutative dg model of $A$
is quasi-isomorphic to
$\Lambda(\epsilon)$, $|\epsilon|=1$: a cocycle representing this
basis defines a map from the free graded-commutative algebra on
one odd generator, and the known cohomology in degrees $0,1$
makes it a quasi-isomorphism.  We use this as a model choice for
this calculation only; no Galois-equivariant trivialization of a
Tate line is claimed.

Write $A_0=\Lambda(\epsilon)$ and
$A_0^e=\Lambda(\epsilon_l,\epsilon_r)$, with the graded opposite
convention.  A semifree $A_0^e$-resolution of its diagonal module is
\[
 F=A_0^e\otimes\Q[t],\qquad |t|=0,\qquad
 dt=\epsilon_l-\epsilon_r,
 \qquad F\longrightarrow A_0,\quad t\longmapsto0.
\]
The filtration by polynomial degree is semifree, since the
differential lowers that degree.  To check the augmentation, use
coordinates $\epsilon_r$ and $\delta=\epsilon_l-\epsilon_r$.
The remaining complex is
$\Q[t]\xrightarrow{\partial_t}\delta\Q[t]$.
In characteristic zero its kernel is the constants and its
cokernel is zero, proving the quasi-isomorphism.

Set $t^{[n]}=t^n/n!$.  Then $dt^{[n]}=\delta t^{[n-1]}$.
On the diagonal module $\delta$ acts by zero, whence
\[
 \RHom_{A_0^e}(A_0,A_0)
   \simeq\operatorname{Hom}_{A_0^e}(F,A_0)
   =\prod_{n\geq0} A_0,
\]
with zero differential.  The product is essential: $F$ is a direct
sum of free generators all in degree zero, and ordinary derived
Hom takes that sum to a product.  The resolution diagonal
$t\mapsto t\otimes1+1\otimes t$ gives
$t^{[n]}\mapsto\sum_{i+j=n}t^{[i]}\otimes t^{[j]}$.
Thus the cup product on degree-zero sequences is the power-series
product, giving $H^0=\Q[[z]]$.  This is the usual derived Hom,
not a polynomial or a separately imposed continuous variant.

The degree-zero map from the geometric source sends scalars to
scalar multiples of the identity, hence to the constant series.
It is not surjective.  Finally,
Theorem~\ref{thm:anchored-localization} identifies the localized
source with its full generated anchor source, so it does not add
these missing endomorphisms to $I_U$.  The discrepancy persists
after localization.  It describes additional coherent natural
transformations in the Morita action category, and is compatible
with all the strong composition assertions above.
\end{proof}

\section{Scope of the result}
The source is generated by the named geometric nearby kernels and
all their actual morphisms.  Inverting its refinement graphs gives
model independence only for $xy=t^4$, $B=\{2\}$ and the four stated
models, with complete inverse-image supports.  The two rectangular
convolutions and their nontrivial cochain factors are retained in
this localization; it is not a category consisting only of invertible
operations.  The cellular target retains arbitrary coarse attachments
and right-target-perfect bimodules, without asserting smoothness of
the cellular algebras or full faithfulness of kernel realization.

No original log/root kernel realization, integral coefficient extension,
external infinite base inertia, arbitrary fine support, change of anchor
or unrestricted product-kernel equivalence is included.  These would
require additional geometric comparison theorems.  The present statement
supplies a fixed anchored, nearby-enhanced kernel and Verdier interface;
it does not by itself construct the full orbital correspondence or
model-independent Grand Theorem.

Appendix~\ref{sec:root-interface} proves only the local completed-root
realization, the two coarse rectangular tests and the selected carrier
comparison.  Appendix~\ref{sec:root-obstruction} excludes ordinary
stalk/pullback gluing of the distribution unit.  The actual exceptional
mate retains the full supported dualizing complex; it does not construct
a global ordinary shriek kernel calculus.  That construction is a separate
research problem, not an unproved part of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}.
In particular the nearby result does not depend on solving it.
Proposition~\ref{prop:hochschild-boundary} also rules out promoting the
stated Morita realization to a full kernel equivalence on the open
stratum.  Neither adding models nor changing the $\log$ notation supplies
the missing root interface.

$\Gamma=\mathbf Z_\ell(1)$, $G_n=\Gamma/\ell^n\Gamma$ and

independent relative inertia factors \emph{after} this neutralization;

For $\Gamma_a=0$ or $\Gamma$, put
$A_{a,r}=R\Gamma_{\mathrm{cts}}(\Gamma_a,\Lambda_r)$ and
$I_{a,r}=C_{\mathrm{lc}}(\Gamma_a,\Lambda_r)$.  The inflation assembly of the finite permutation diagonals is
$C_{\mathrm{lc}}(\Gamma_a,\Lambda_r)$; continuous Shapiro identifies

\begin{proposition}[A node passage distinguishes star and trace assembly]
\label{prop:root-node-integral-test}
At a node, continuous cohomological integration of the constant
coefficient gives $A_r=C^*_{\mathrm{cts}}(\Gamma,\Lambda_r)$.
In contrast, assembling the actual finite-stack shriek images
along their trace transitions gives
\[
 J_r:=R\varprojlim_n Rg_{n,!}\Lambda_r
       \simeq R\operatorname{Hom}_{\Lambda_r}(A_r,\Lambda_r),
 \qquad g_n:BG_n\to\operatorname{Spec}k.
\]
After adic recovery and rationalization, $A$ has cohomology in
$(0,1)$ whereas $J=D_{\Q}A$ has cohomology in $(-1,0)$.
Consequently trace-assembled shriek convolution of the two constant
root rectangles through this node does not give the nearby
rectangle convolution factor $A$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
At finite precision, \cite[\texttt{prop:frag-finite-quotient-enhancement}]{Local}
identifies the actual geometric shriek adjunction with derived
coinvariants, including its counit and composition cells.
Its \texttt{prop:frag-point-geometric-bars} identifies the full bar
and its geometric pairing.  Thus the specified comparison is
\[
 Rg_{n,!}\Lambda_r
 \simeq\Lambda_r\otimes^L_{\Lambda_r[G_n]}\Lambda_r
 \simeq D_{\Lambda_r}C^*(G_n,\Lambda_r).
\]
Under this comparison the quotient trace is the dual of inflation,
with the coefficient arrows specified by
\cite[\texttt{lem:frag-point-trace-diagram-descent}]{Local}.
These are unbounded full bars; $BG_n$ has dimension-zero dualizing
coefficient $\Lambda_r$.  Therefore
\[
 R\varprojlim_n D_{\Lambda_r}C^*(G_n,\Lambda_r)
   \simeq D_{\Lambda_r}\operatorname*{hocolim}_n C^*(G_n,\Lambda_r)
   =D_{\Lambda_r}A_r.
\]
There is no truncation of the unbounded finite bars in this step.
The resulting $A_r$ is the perfect two-term continuous complex;
its dual is likewise perfect and compatible with derived precision
reduction.  Recovering $r$ and then rationalizing gives
$H^{-1}(J)=\Q(1)$, $H^0(J)=\Q$, whereas
$H^0(A)=\Q$, $H^1(A)=\Q(-1)$.

For the passage $U\to q\to U$, the two constant root rectangles
have constant middle coefficient.  Finite-stack convolution thus
integrates that coefficient by $Rg_{n,!}$.  Retaining the trace
assembly gives the scalar factor $J$, multiplying the unchanged
outer rectangular coefficient.  Near the node the convolution in
Proposition~\ref{prop:rectangle} instead has factor $A$.
An exact $\Q$-linear realization that restricts to the identity
kernel functor on the root-free open stratum (and hence on its
scalar-tensored rectangular kernels), and sends these two constant
rectangles to the named
nearby rectangles cannot identify these composites.  This is an
obstruction to this \emph{specific} naive choice of shriek assembly
and rectangles, not to every possible normalized root source.
The finite-carrier supported trace in \cite{Local} has, in addition,
its carrier shift and twist; it cannot remove this distinction by
being renamed ordinary inertia integration.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Completed shriek kernels and the source normalization}
\label{sec:completed-shriek-normalization}
We now resolve the node-integration discrepancy for the specified
rectangular kernels.  The endpoints in the point-valued calculation carry
inertia $0$ or $\Gamma=\mathbf Z_\ell(1)$, as occurs on strata
of the fixed models.  An endpoint with zero inertia is a point;
it is not the actual open scheme $U=\mathbf G_m$.  No statement for general
root stacks is needed.  The result is local on these strata.  A global kernel calculus is
not obtained by gluing these modules through ordinary stalk pullback;
the obstruction is proved in Appendix~\ref{sec:root-obstruction}.

Put $E_{a,r}=\Lambda_r$ with trivial inertia, and
$R_{a,r}=\Lambda_r[[\Gamma_a]]$, the Iwasawa (measure-type)
completed group algebra, not an algebra of locally analytic
distributions.  Use the derived category of
pseudocompact modules, computed by completed free resolutions,
with completed tensor and continuous derived mapping spectra.
Root-level inverse systems are used to present these modules;
we do not identify this category with
$\varprojlim_n D(\Lambda_r[G_n])$ under derived scalar reduction.
In particular the augmentation's finite presentations are not
asserted derived-cartesian over finite group rings.

There are two independent root indices in mapping computations:
a finite quotient presenting a distribution coefficient, and a
possibly larger quotient computing its continuous action.  For a
pseudocompact target $W=\varprojlim_nW_n$, continuous equivariant
Hom is computed by resolving the source and then taking
\[
 R\varprojlim_n C^*_{\mathrm{cts}}
  \bigl(\Gamma_a\times\Gamma_b,
     R\operatorname{Hom}^{\mathrm{cts}}_{\Lambda_r}(V,W_n)\bigr).
\]
The cochains inside are inflation colimits over sufficiently large
action levels, not cochains of the single group presenting $W_n$.
This is the completed equivariant bar model for the mapping spectra
in the preceding definition.  It retains derived continuous maps
which a diagonal finite-level Hom calculation would miss.

\begin{lemma}[The source orientation from the completed augmentation]
\label{lem:root-source-orientation}
The augmentation $E_{a,r}$ is perfect over $R_{a,r}$.  Its relative
dual is
\[
 \mathfrak l_{a,r}:=\RHom_{R_{a,r}}^{\mathrm{cts}}(E_{a,r},R_{a,r})
 \simeq
 \begin{cases}
 \Lambda_r,&\Gamma_a=0,\\
 \Lambda_r(-1)[-1],&\Gamma_a=\Gamma,
 \end{cases}
\]
as a left $R_{a,r}$-module with augmentation action.  Evaluation gives,
for every right module $M$, the canonical natural equivalence
\begin{equation}
 M\widehat\otimes_{R_{a,r}}^L\mathfrak l_{a,r}
  \xrightarrow{\sim}\RHom_{R_{a,r}}^{\mathrm{cts}}(E_{a,r},M).
 \label{eq:root-oriented-evaluation}
\end{equation}
For $M=E_{a,r}$ this identifies the trace factor with the cochain
factor, with both augmentation-endomorphism actions:
\[
 J_{a,r}\otimes\mathfrak l_{a,r}\simeq A_{a,r},\qquad
 J_{a,r}=E_{a,r}\widehat\otimes_{R_{a,r}}^L E_{a,r}.
\]
When used as a scalar tensor factor we write
$\underline{\mathfrak l}_{a,r}$ for the underlying orientation line
of $\mathfrak l_{a,r}$.  The augmentation-endomorphism/cochain actions
are still those transported by the evaluation comparison; the
underline does not replace them by trivial actions.  In the last
formula the tensor is over $\Lambda_r$ with this underlying line;
the augmentation action is retained in the tensor defining $J$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At a node choose the fixed Kummer coordinate and write
$T=\gamma-1$.  The two-term completed free resolution
$[R_{a,r}\xrightarrow{T}R_{a,r}]$ of the augmentation, in degrees
$-1,0$, is exact since $T$ is a non-zero-divisor even over
$\Lambda_r$.  Dualizing it gives the augmentation in degree $1$.
Intrinsically its degree-one line is
$(I/I^2)^\vee=\Lambda_r(-1)$, so the identification has the stated
Tate twist, rather than a basis-dependent scalar shift.
Evaluation of a finite free resolution proves
\eqref{eq:root-oriented-evaluation} for every $M$ and on its full
mapping category.  Taking $M=E_{a,r}$ gives $A_{a,r}$ by the same
augmentation resolution that computes continuous cohomology.
Naturality in the source augmentation and the target $M$ proves
linearity for the two endomorphism actions.  Thus the assertion is
a balanced bimodule comparison, not a choice of an isomorphism
$D A(-1)[-1]\simeq A$ from graded dimensions.  The zero-inertia case
is the regular-module evaluation.

One can see the orientation directly in actual continuous root
coefficients.  For $R_n=\Lambda_r[G_n]$, the continuous action
complex is $[R_n\xrightarrow{T}R_n]$ in degrees $0,1$.
Its degree-zero invariant is the norm element; quotient sends it
to $\ell$ times the preceding norm.  Its degree-one cokernel is
$\Lambda_r(-1)$ and the transition is the identity.  At fixed $r$
the former inverse system is pro-zero and the latter is constant,
so $R\varprojlim_nR\Gamma_{\mathrm{cts}}(\Gamma,R_n)
=\Lambda_r(-1)[-1]$.  The two-term complexes identify this with
$\RHom_R(\Lambda_r,R)$, including its maps.  In contrast
$R\operatorname{Hom}_{\Lambda_r[G_n]}(\Lambda_r,R_n)$ at that
single finite group level has no such degree-one class.  This
exhibits why the continuous-action index cannot be silently tied
to the distribution index.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[The completed local shriek generating source]
\label{def:completed-root-shriek-source}
For endpoints $a,b$ with the preceding inertia, take actual
pseudocompact $(R_{a,r},R_{b,r})$-bimodules, retaining their continuous
derived mapping spectra.  Composition is completed derived tensor
over the middle algebra.  Its unit is the regular bimodule $R_{a,r}$.
The normalized rectangular generator is
\[
 Q_{ab,r}=\mathfrak l_{a,r}\widehat\otimes_{\Lambda_r}E_{b,r}.
\]
Take the smallest full stable idempotent-complete unital convolution
source generated by \emph{all} regular diagonal bimodules $R_{a,r}$
and \emph{all} normalized rectangles $Q_{ab,r}$, with perfect scalar
coefficients.  Inertia graph bimodules are not additional generators.  The two
endpoints remain in their original order.  The shift belongs to
the \emph{source} factor, and no shift is inserted in the unit.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Local completed shriek realization]
\label{thm:completed-root-shriek}
On this source there is an exact enhanced realization on full
kernel mapping categories,
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal R_{!,r}^{\mathrm{loc}}(P)
 =\RHom_{R_{b,r}}^{\mathrm{cts}}
       (E_{b,r},E_{a,r}\widehat\otimes_{R_{a,r}}^L P),
 \label{eq:completed-root-realization}
\end{equation}
valued in $(A_{a,r},A_{b,r})$-bimodules perfect on the right.  It
sends the completed actual diagonal to the regular unit and the
normalized rectangle to the ordinary nearby rectangular bimodule:
\[
 \mathcal R_{!,r}^{\mathrm{loc}}(R_{a,r})\simeq A_{a,r},\qquad
 \mathcal R_{!,r}^{\mathrm{loc}}(Q_{ab,r})
       \simeq A_{a,r}\otimes_{\Lambda_r}A_{b,r}.
\]
It has the canonical strong convolution constraint
\[
 \mathcal R_{!,r}^{\mathrm{loc}}(P)\otimes_{A_{b,r}}^L
 \mathcal R_{!,r}^{\mathrm{loc}}(L)
   \xrightarrow{\sim}\mathcal R_{!,r}^{\mathrm{loc}}
                              (P\widehat\otimes_{R_{b,r}}^L L).
\]
The diagonal and rectangle convolution constraints are the
continuous assemblies of the actual finite-root shriek operations
specified in the proof, not continuous invariants substituted for
those operations.
The same statements hold for the derived cartesian integral systems
generated by these kernels, and after their rationalization: precision
reduction is performed on the augmentation resolutions, followed by
adic recovery.  No finite root level is rationalized first.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For the actual finite diagonal use the distribution basis of its
permutation module $\Lambda_r[G_n]$.  The transition
$[g]\mapsto[\bar g]$ is quotient-fibre summation on distributions.
It is the dual of pullback of finite functions, hence is the trace
mate for the finite diagonal correspondence.  This assertion is
on the residual gerbe.  Across a normal branch its geometric mate
has the exceptional/costalk type described in
Proposition~\ref{prop:root-diagonal-branch-transition}; it is not a
map to the ordinary pullback of the preceding diagonal kernel.  Its inverse limit
is $R_{a,r}$ with both regular actions.  Unlike the inflation
assembly $I_{a,r}$, this is the unit of the completed shriek source.
The finite-level identity contractions
$\Lambda_r[G_n]\otimes_{\Lambda_r[G_n]}P_n\to P_n$ commute with
these quotient maps, including the contracting homotopies of the
regular bar.  Thus they give the unit constraints after assembly.

A normalized rectangle has the actual finite-root predecessor
$\Lambda_r(-1)[-1]\boxtimes\Lambda_r$ if its source is a node,
and the unshifted constant rectangle if the source inertia is zero.
Its trace transition is the identity on these coefficient lines.
This is a chosen shifted kernel coefficient, not the finite-group
shriek image itself.  For two rectangles the finite middle shriek
operation is the full group-homology bar, with its actual trace maps
under quotient, using the geometric input chain in
Proposition~\ref{prop:root-node-integral-test}.
Its inverse assembly is $J_{b,r}$ by
Proposition~\ref{prop:root-node-integral-test}.  The completed
augmentation resolution computes the same $J_{b,r}$, and the
comparison of the completed bar with that resolution identifies
the evaluation map.  Consequently
\begin{equation}
 Q_{ab,r}\widehat\otimes_{R_{b,r}}^L Q_{bc,r}
  \simeq A_{b,r}\otimes_{\Lambda_r}Q_{ac,r}.
 \label{eq:normalized-root-rectangles}
\end{equation}
The map is precisely \eqref{eq:root-oriented-evaluation} on the
middle factor.  The two outer actions are unchanged.  For any fixed
finite word of rectangles, the finite computation uses a finite
product of full homology bars.  Dualizing gives finite products of
cohomology bars, whose inflation colimit is the corresponding
continuous product bar; each resulting continuous complex is
perfect over $\Lambda_r$.  Dualizing back identifies the trace
assembly with the completed tensor computation.  No infinite
root bar is truncated and no inverse limit is moved through an
arbitrary unbounded tensor.  Regular factors are handled by their
coherent bar contractions.  This proves the claimed geometric
assembly on the specified local convolution words.  For diagrams
of their actual continuous maps, retain the independent action and
distribution indices described above.  Exactness extends an already
constructed comparison of such diagrams to its cones and retracts;
the ambient realization itself is defined on every continuous
kernel map by \eqref{eq:completed-root-realization}.

The selected coefficient category at $a$ is
$\operatorname{thick}_{R_{a,r}}(E_{a,r})$, with its actual continuous
module maps.  For $Q_{ab,r}$ its action on $M$ is
\[
 M\widehat\otimes_{R_{a,r}}^L Q_{ab,r}
   \simeq \RHom_{R_{a,r}}^{\mathrm{cts}}(E_{a,r},M)
                 \otimes E_{b,r}.
\]
For $M$ in this thick category the scalar factor is perfect:
check $M=E_{a,r}$ by the finite augmentation resolution, then use
finite cones and retracts.  Thus every generator, and consequently
every generated kernel, preserves the selected coefficient types.
Formula \eqref{eq:completed-root-realization} is therefore its
Morita bimodule coordinate, defined by the actual completed kernel
action.  The source endomorphisms act through this action and the
target endomorphisms by precomposition.  Evaluation on the middle
augmentation and thick generation prove the strong tensor
comparison, exactly as in Proposition~\ref{thm:kernel-morita}.
For a rectangle its value is $A_{a,r}\otimes A_{b,r}$ by
\eqref{eq:root-oriented-evaluation}; for a unit it is $A_{a,r}$
with the regular actions.  All composition and unit cells are the
ones induced by tensor evaluation and the completed bar, so they
are natural on the full mapping categories.  No full faithfulness
of this realization is required.  The unit, orientation and rectangle
systems commute with derived precision reduction: their completed
free augmentation resolutions reduce to the corresponding resolutions
over $\Lambda_r$.  Their realized images are perfect scalar complexes
with uniformly bounded amplitude for each finite expression.  Finite
cones, convolution and retracts of these cartesian systems preserve
the stated comparisons.  Their adic limits and subsequent inversion
of $\ell$ therefore give the final assertion; no claim concerns
arbitrary precision-dependent infinite convolution words.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Two geometric coarse-passage tests and carrier correction]
\label{prop:normalized-root-passage}
At fixed precision put
\[
 Q_{Uq}=\Lambda_{r,U}\boxtimes E_q,\qquad
 Q_{qU}=\mathfrak l_q\boxtimes\Lambda_{r,U},\qquad
 Q_{UU}=\Lambda_{r,U}\boxtimes\Lambda_{r,U},\qquad
 Q_{qq}=\mathfrak l_q\otimes_{\Lambda_r}E_q.
\]
In the separate test environment just specified, the two compositions
are
\begin{align*}
 Q_{qU}\star Q_{Uq}&\simeq A_q\otimes_{\Lambda_r}Q_{UU},\\
 Q_{Uq}\star Q_{qU}&\simeq R\Gamma_c(U,\Lambda_r)
                                      \otimes_{\Lambda_r}Q_{qq}.
\end{align*}
The actual unit on $U$ is $\Delta_{U,*}\Lambda_r$, not $Q_{UU}$.
There are separate factorwise comparisons with the specified nearby
rectangles: target augmentation Hom on $Q_{Uq}$ gives
$\Lambda_{r,U}\boxtimes A_q$; source augmentation evaluation on
$Q_{qU}$ gives $A_q\boxtimes\Lambda_{r,U}$, with its source cochain
action.  After the selected adic passage and rationalization these
are $H_U\boxtimes A_q$ and $A_q\boxtimes H_U$ through the actual
cochain-to-nearby comparisons.  These object and action comparisons
are not denoted by $\mathcal R_!^{\mathrm{loc}}$, whose defined source
is point-valued.  They identify the two displayed geometric passage
factors, not a new common enhanced functor.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For $U\to q\to U$ the middle trace factor is
$J_q\otimes_{\Lambda_r}\underline{\mathfrak l}_q$; its specified
augmentation evaluation gives $A_q$.  The two outer sheaves remain
on the actual product $U\times U$.  For $q\to U\to q$, ordinary
scheme shriek integration of the middle constant sheaf gives
$R\Gamma_c(U,\Lambda_r)$, leaving the outer $Q_{qq}$.  This factor
has nonzero cohomology in degrees one and two; replacing $U$ by the
zero-inertia point would instead give $\Lambda_r$ and would change
the calculation.

The first factorwise comparison is the actual map
\[
 \RHom_{R_q}(E_q,\Lambda_{r,U}\boxtimes E_q)
       \simeq\Lambda_{r,U}\boxtimes A_q.
\]
For its action on $N\in\operatorname{thick}(\Lambda_{r,U})$, it identifies
$K_q(R\Gamma_c(U,N)\otimes E_q)$ with
$R\Gamma_c(U,N)\otimes A_q$.  Here $R\Gamma_c(U,N)$ is scalar
perfect in the selected geometric domain, so the tensor--Hom
comparison follows from the finite augmentation resolution.
For the reverse rectangle the action on a selected node coefficient
$M$ is
\[
 (M\widehat\otimes_{R_q}\mathfrak l_q)\boxtimes\Lambda_{r,U}
       \xrightarrow{\sim}K_q(M)\boxtimes\Lambda_{r,U}.
\]
This is precisely \eqref{eq:root-oriented-evaluation}, retaining
the source $A_q$ action.  It is the action of the independently
specified nearby rectangle $A_q\boxtimes\Lambda_{r,U}$ on $K_q(M)$.
These comparisons come from the node factor of the actual exterior
kernels; the geometric $U$ factor and its support have not been
replaced by point-valued Morita coordinates.  Inserting these maps
in the two contractions gives the middle factors in the statement.
All assertions are natural for the coefficient maps in these
expressions.  Compatible systems obtained from fixed finite
expressions admit the same precision reduction, adic recovery and
last-step rationalization as the local calculation.  This does not
construct a functor on arbitrary mixed convolution words or their
full kernel mapping categories.

For comparison with the first paper, a rank-$N$ affine carrier has
shriek factor $c_N=\Lambda_r(-N)[-2N]$.  Its trace-assembled node
output is $J_q\otimes c_N$, whereas the bare-node normalized
rectangle uses $J_q\otimes\mathfrak l_q$.  To compute the latter
using that carrier one must use the actual coefficient line
\begin{equation}
 \mathfrak l_q\otimes c_N^{-1}
       =\Lambda_r(N-1)[2N-1]
 \label{eq:root-carrier-compensation}
\end{equation}
on the carrier.  The vector-bundle trace, with that line, gives
$J_q\otimes c_N\otimes(\mathfrak l_q c_N^{-1})
=J_q\otimes\mathfrak l_q$.  For $N=1$ the compensating line is
$\Lambda_r[1]$, not the bare-node line $\Lambda_r(-1)[-1]$.
Projection for this invertible coefficient proves the comparison
on maps and pairings.  This explains precisely which carrier
normalization connects its supported trace with nearby ordinary
node integration; an uncompensated carrier trace remains different.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Inertia graph coefficients in the ambient completed category]
\label{prop:completed-root-graphs}
For a stratum map in one of the fixed contractions, let
$\varphi:\Gamma_b\to\Gamma_a$ be its actual neutralized inertia
map.  The completed transposed graph coefficient is
\[
 {}_{R_{a,r}}R_{a,r}{}_{R_{b,r}},
\]
with right action induced by $\varphi$.  This is an object of the
ambient completed bimodule category, not in general of the source
of Definition~\ref{def:completed-root-shriek-source}.
Its selected coefficient action is restriction along $\varphi$.
For this additional object the same coordinate expression is
separately defined by
\[
 \mathsf M_r(P_\varphi):=
 \RHom_{R_{b,r}}(E_{b,r},E_{a,r}\widehat\otimes^L_{R_{a,r}}P_\varphi).
\]
It is $A_{b,r}$ with the source action through the actual cochain map
$A_{a,r}\to A_{b,r}$.  These comparisons respect composition of
the fixed inertia maps and all kernel morphisms.  They include the
rank-drop case $\Gamma_b=0$, $\Gamma_a=\Gamma$.  This assertion
concerns graph restriction actions and their graph-only composition;
it does not extend Theorem~\ref{thm:completed-root-shriek} to a source
generated jointly by graphs and rectangles.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
At a finite quotient the actual graph of $B\varphi$ on the product
has the permutation coefficient on the corresponding graph cosets.
Viewed as a left/right bimodule this is the regular source group
algebra with the right target action through $\varphi$.
Quotient-fibre trace maps give its completed coefficient as above.
Its tensor action is $M\widehat\otimes_{R_a}R_a$, with the restricted
right $R_b$-action, so it sends the augmentation to the augmentation
and preserves its thick closure.  Evaluating $\mathsf M_r(P_\varphi)$ gives
$\RHom_{R_b}(E_b,E_b)=A_b$.  Naturality of the restriction functor
on augmentation endomorphisms gives the left action by the actual
continuous cochain restriction, not an arbitrary algebra map with
the same cohomology.  For a composite $\Gamma_c\to\Gamma_b\to\Gamma_a$
the kernel comparison is the regular cancellation
$R_a\widehat\otimes_{R_b}R_b\to R_a$.  Its tensor associativity
and unit maps are already the finite graph composition maps and
remain so after completion.  This proves the local coefficient
faces of the fixed refinement diamond, without proving gluing on
the complete exceptional fibres.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Why the ambient graph is an additional object]
At $r=1$, a word from $\Gamma$ to $0$ in the point-valued generating
source must contain a rectangle.  Cancelling regular units and using
\eqref{eq:normalized-root-rectangles} leaves a perfect scalar factor
times that rectangle.  Thus its underlying $\mathbf F_\ell$ complex
is perfect; finite cones and retracts preserve this property.  The
rank-drop graph ${}_{\mathbf F_\ell[[T]]}\mathbf F_\ell[[T]]{}_{\mathbf F_\ell}$
has infinite-dimensional degree-zero cohomology and is not an object
of that Hom category.  Adding regular units explicitly does not
remove this distinction.
\end{remark}

\begin{proposition}[Local evaluation and normalized trace]
\label{prop:normalized-root-pairing}
The node normalization is compatible with the actual trace-dual
pairing.  Explicitly, if $\eta:J_q\otimes\mathfrak l_q\to A_q$
is \eqref{eq:root-oriented-evaluation}, the normalized pairing is
\[
 A_q\otimes A_q\xrightarrow{\eta^{-1}\otimes1}
 J_q\otimes\mathfrak l_q\otimes A_q
       \xrightarrow{\operatorname{ev}\otimes1}\mathfrak l_q.
\]
It is balanced for the two cochain actions and nondegenerate, with
their graded side conventions.  Before normalization the trace
pairing has target $\Lambda_r$; the carrier pairing has target
$c_N$.  Tensoring by \eqref{eq:root-carrier-compensation} gives
exactly the displayed target $\mathfrak l_q$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Compute the first evaluation with the finite free augmentation
resolution and its dual.  The two contraction orders are the same
tensor--Hom evaluation: moving an augmentation endomorphism across
the contraction precomposes one factor and postcomposes the other.
This proves balancing and naturality on the full endomorphism
mapping category.  The completed bar comparison identifies the
other contraction with the dual of inflation, hence with the
finite-root trace assembly, as in
Proposition~\ref{prop:root-node-integral-test}.  The orientation map
is evaluation of that same resolution, so it does not change the
pairing by an unspecified scalar.  The resulting map pairs degree
zero with degree one; those two free lines are perfect duals with
value $\Lambda_r(-1)[-1]$.  Evaluation and coevaluation obey their
usual triangle identities.  Proper vector-bundle trace and scalar
projection give the last assertion, retaining its twist and shift.
These are local pairings on the selected inertia charts; no
coefficient duality across an attached whole fibre is inferred.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Comparison with the actual three-realization carrier trace}
\label{sec:carrier-interface}
Write $\mathsf T_{N,r}(V)$ for the actual trace-assembled rank-$N$
carrier functor of \cite{Local}, not for a newly defined invariants
functor.  Its coefficient arrows and trace maps are those of\\
\texttt{lem:frag-point-trace-diagram-descent} there.  Write
$K_r(M)=R\operatorname{Hom}^{\mathrm{cts}}_R(E_r,M)$ and
$J_r(M)=M\widehat\otimes_R^LE_r$.

\begin{theorem}[The normalized carrier interface on selected coefficients]
\label{thm:selected-carrier-interface}
Let $M\in\operatorname{thick}_R(E_r)$ and put
$d_{N,r}=\mathfrak l_r c_N^{-1}=\Lambda_r(N-1)[2N-1]$.
There is a natural equivalence of the actual functors
\begin{equation}
 \Theta_{N,M}:\mathsf T_{N,r}(M\otimes d_{N,r})
        \xrightarrow{\sim} K_r(M).
 \label{eq:selected-carrier-interface}
\end{equation}
It respects the augmentation-cochain action, evaluation, the actual
root trace transitions and coefficient-reduction maps.  It is
natural on the full selected mapping category, not only for the
constant coefficient.  It therefore intertwines the carrier
comparison with the kernel actions of
Theorem~\ref{thm:completed-root-shriek}, including their maps and
composition constraints; at a zero-inertia point use the identity
coefficient comparison.  This does not assert a common realization
for the ambient graph objects or the geometric coarse-passage tests.

Adic recovery and subsequent rationalization concern only compatible
systems $\{M_r\}_r$ equipped with specified derived precision-reduction
equivalences and the uniformly bounded scalar-perfect presentations
used here, for instance systems constructed by one fixed finite
generating expression with compatible maps and retracts.  The
augmentation comparisons and carrier coefficients use those same
reduction maps.  A single fixed-precision $M$, or unrelated choices
of $M_r$, is not asserted to admit such a recovery.

For the fixed node carrier $N=1$, the coefficient supplied to the
first paper's supported realization is $M[1]$.  Its actual supported
analytic and nearby costalk realizations of $M[1]$ are thereby
identified with the ordinary nearby cochain realization of $M$.
This is a comparison of these selected point-valued functors, not
an equality of stalk and costalk functors on arbitrary attached
objects.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every object in $\operatorname{thick}_R(E_r)$ is perfect over
$\Lambda_r$: this holds for $E_r$ and is preserved by finite cones
and retracts.  It therefore belongs to the point coefficient domain
of the first paper.  Its actual trace comparison gives
\[
 \phi_V:\mathsf T_{N,r}(V)
    \xrightarrow{\sim}D_{\Lambda_r}K_r(D_{\Lambda_r}V)\otimes c_N
    \simeq J_r(V)\otimes c_N.
\]
The last map is evaluation on the same finite free completed
augmentation resolution; contragredient actions and the graded
side exchanges are retained.  In particular this use of the first
paper is legitimate for $M$, but would not be legitimate for the
infinite-rank kernel $R$ regarded as a coefficient.
Projection for the invertible scalar line $d_{N,r}$ and
\eqref{eq:root-oriented-evaluation} now give the specified composite
\[
 \mathsf T_{N,r}(M\otimes d_{N,r})
  \xrightarrow{\phi}\ J_r(M)\otimes c_N\otimes d_{N,r}
  =J_r(M)\otimes\mathfrak l_r
  \xrightarrow{\operatorname{ev}}K_r(M).
\]
This defines $\Theta$ on maps, including its module action.  The
last action is the augmentation-endomorphism action on the relative
dual $\RHom_R(E_r,R)$; it is not discarded when its underlying
orientation line is written as $\Lambda_r(-1)[-1]$.
Both pairings are contractions of the augmentation bar with its
dual, and the finite-root pairing is precisely the one identified
by \texttt{lem:frag-point-trace-diagram-descent}.  Thus balancing
identifies the first paper's transpose cup action with this action.
This proves the module assertion and fixes the map, not merely its
value as a scalar complex.

For a coefficient map $f:M\to M'$, the square is explicitly
\[
\begin{CD}
 \mathsf T_{N,r}(M\otimes d_{N,r}) @>{\Theta_{N,M}}>> K_r(M)\\
 @V{\mathsf T_{N,r}(f\otimes1)}VV @VV{K_r(f)}V\\
 \mathsf T_{N,r}(M'\otimes d_{N,r}) @>{\Theta_{N,M'}}>> K_r(M').
\end{CD}
\]
It is the square of evaluation and the actual trace functor on
mapping categories.  The trace transitions in a chosen presentation
satisfy $e_n(t_{m,n}\otimes1)=e_m(1\otimes\mathrm{inf}_{n,m})$;
tensoring by $d_N$ and applying the same evaluation preserves that
identity.  The coefficient-reduction square is the actual square
of the cited lemma, followed by reduction of the finite free
augmentation resolution.  This proves compatibility with both
indices before recovery.

For a kernel $P$ in Definition~\ref{def:completed-root-shriek-source},
its action $F_P(M)=M\widehat\otimes_R P$
lands in the target selected category.  Substitution of $F_P(M)$
into the square above proves compatibility with coefficient maps
and kernel maps.  For two kernels substitute the actual tensor
associativity map $F_LF_P(M)\to F_{P\otimes L}(M)$; naturality
identifies the two carrier comparisons along this map.  The same
substitution on its higher simplices gives their coherence.  At
the point this is a proved interface for the local kernel actions,
not an application of the first paper to growing diagonal kernels.

Finally \texttt{cor:frag-C2-local-closed} of \cite{Local} identifies
$\mathsf T_{N,r}(V)$ with the actual supported analytic and nearby
objects, using the same dual star comparison and source twist.
Apply it to $V=M\otimes d_N$.  This proves the last assertion for
$N=1$.  We have not invoked that paper's separate orientation-factor
formula outside its stated trivial-character domain; here the
comparison for nontrivial selected $M$ is proved by relative
augmentation evaluation.  For the compatible systems specified in
the statement, these perfect reductions supply integral recovery,
followed by rationalization; no lift of an isolated $M$ is inferred.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Ordered completion and coupled inertia}
\begin{lemma}[The two root indices cannot be replaced by the diagonal]
\label{lem:root-index-obstruction}
At fixed $r$ set $R_n=\Lambda_r[G_n]$, with distribution quotient
transitions.  The correct completed equivariant computation is
\[
 R\varprojlim_n R\Gamma_{\mathrm{cts}}(\Gamma,R_n)
       \simeq\Lambda_r(-1)[-1].
\]
Replacing continuous cohomology by the cohomology of the same
finite group presenting $R_n$ gives instead a pro-zero system of
norm lines, hence zero inverse limit.  Thus such a replacement
loses a nonzero derived map, not merely an optional coherence choice.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The completed augmentation resolution gives
$[R_n\xrightarrow{T}R_n(-1)]$ in degrees $0,1$, with the
coordinate-free interpretation of the twist.  Its kernel is the
norm line.  The quotient map sends the norm at level $n+1$ to
$\ell$ times the norm at level $n$, so this kernel system is
pro-zero at fixed $r$.  Its cokernel is $\Lambda_r(-1)$ with
identity transition.  These systems are Mittag--Leffler; the
inverse limit is the displayed degree-one line.

For the alternative calculation the regular finite module $R_n$
is coinduced from the trivial subgroup of $G_n$.  Hence
$R\Gamma(G_n,R_n)$ is its invariant norm line in degree zero,
with no positive cohomology.  The maps on these norm lines induced
by distribution quotient are multiplication by $\ell$ and have
zero derived inverse limit.  At a fixed distribution level $n$,
continuous cohomology instead requires inflation over all larger
action levels with $R_n$ inflated as coefficient.  That additional
limit direction supplies the degree-one class.  The replacement
just tested omits it.  In particular the orientation morphisms and
product attaching maps cannot be certified by testing only the
single-level finite groups.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Coupled inertia of the completed diagonal]
\label{prop:root-coupled-inertia}
Fix a node and a precision $r$.  Write $R=\Lambda_r[[\Gamma]]$
and let $R_n=\Lambda_r[G_n]$ carry the left/right action of
$\Gamma\times\Gamma$ through $(g,h)[a]=[gah^{-1}]$.  There is a
specified equivalence
\begin{equation}
 R\varprojlim_n R\Gamma_{\mathrm{cts}}
       (\Gamma\times\Gamma,R_n)
       \simeq \mathfrak l\otimes_{\Lambda_r} A_r,
 \label{eq:root-coupled-inertia}
\end{equation}
where the second factor is the cochain algebra of the diagonal
stabilizer.  The first factor belongs to the quotient
$(\Gamma\times\Gamma)/\Delta\Gamma$, identified with the source
inertia by $(g,h)\mapsto gh^{-1}$.  In particular the two inertia
directions do not contribute two independent regular-module
orientation lines.

More generally, for an actual stratum map
$\varphi:\Gamma_b\to\Gamma_a$, the completed graph coefficient
$P_\varphi={}_{R_a}R_a{}_{R_b}$ satisfies
\[
 R\Gamma_{\mathrm{cts}}(\Gamma_a\times\Gamma_b,P_\varphi)
       \simeq\mathfrak l_a\otimes A_b.
\]
Here cohomology with a completed coefficient means the ordered
continuous-action/distribution assembly.  Cancellation of the
source orientation by augmentation evaluation gives precisely
$A_b$ with its actual $A_a$-action in
Proposition~\ref{prop:completed-root-graphs}.
These are ambient equivariant coefficient comparisons.  The graph
coordinate is the separately defined $\mathsf M_r(P_\varphi)$,
not a claim that the graph belongs to the point-valued generating
source.  No geometric product attaching map is implicit in them.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The actual group isomorphism
\[
 \Gamma_a\times\Gamma_b\longrightarrow\Gamma_a\times\Gamma_b,
 \qquad (g,h)\longmapsto(g\varphi(h)^{-1},h)
\]
identifies the graph subgroup with the second factor and its
quotient with the first.  In these coordinates the first factor
acts regularly on $P_\varphi$ and the second acts trivially.
The continuous bar on this product, or its finite completed
augmentation resolution, therefore computes the displayed
cohomology as $\mathfrak l_a\otimes A_b$.  The resolution has
finite length and finite free terms.  Consequently its application
to the distribution system commutes with its derived inverse
limit; no direct/inverse interchange is involved.  At every fixed
distribution presentation its cochains still use all dominating
action levels.

For the diagonal, a generator gives the two-variable cochain
Koszul differential determined by
\[
 T,\qquad (1+T)^{-1}-1=-T(1+T)^{-1}.
\]
The invertible elementary change of these two generators turns
this into $T,0$.  The $T$ direction has inverse limit
$\mathfrak l$ by Lemma~\ref{lem:root-index-obstruction}; the zero
direction is the cochain complex of the diagonal stabilizer.
Keeping the cotangent lines records its Tate twist and the sign
from the inverse right action.  This calculation identifies the
map coming from the actual group isomorphism, rather than merely
its graded dimensions.

Finally, source augmentation evaluation identifies, naturally in
any completed bimodule $P$,
\[
 \RHom_{R_a}(E_a,P)
   \simeq (E_a\widehat\otimes_{R_a}^L P)
                  \otimes_{\Lambda_r}\underline{\mathfrak l}_a.
\]
Applying target augmentation Hom identifies the preceding coupled
calculation with \eqref{eq:completed-root-realization}, after
cancelling this orientation line.  The source and target cochain
actions are retained throughout by precomposition; for a graph
they give the restriction induced by $\varphi$.  Thus the comparison
is the graph realization map already specified, including its
bimodule structure.  Group composition and the relative bar
augmentation give the composition maps, not a separately chosen
splitting of the two-term cohomology.
\end{proof}

\section{An obstruction to ordinary distribution gluing}
\label{sec:root-obstruction}
The ordinary-pullback inverse gluing route is excluded by an actual
attaching square.  The result does not affect the nearby theorem;
it diagnoses a different source construction.

\begin{proposition}[The actual diagonal branch transition and its mate]
\label{prop:root-diagonal-branch-transition}
Let $\mathcal X_n=[D_{z_n}/G_n]$ be a smooth strictly local root
branch, $x=z_n^{\ell^n}$, and let
$q:\mathcal X_m\to\mathcal X_n$, $m>n$, be its actual transition.
Put $F_n=\Delta_{\mathcal X_n,!}\Lambda_r$.  There is a canonical
map
\begin{equation}
 \beta_{n,m}:(q\times q)^*F_n\longrightarrow F_m
 \label{eq:root-diagonal-branch-beta}
\end{equation}
which is the identity on the open diagonal.  On the closed
atlas fibre it is the function-inflation map
\[
 \iota_{n,m}:\Lambda_r[G_n]\longrightarrow\Lambda_r[G_m],
 \qquad [a]\longmapsto\sum_{\bar b=a}[b].
\]
This identifies the coupled diagonal attaching map on this branch,
including its degree-zero product-open link face.  The maps
$\beta$ compose by the actual adjunction comparison.

Its geometric Verdier mate is an actual trace map
\begin{equation}
 (q\times q)_!D F_m\longrightarrow D F_n.
 \label{eq:root-diagonal-branch-trace}
\end{equation}
Since these branch stacks are smooth of dimension one,
$D F_n=F_n(1)[2]$.  Thus the mate also gives
$(q\times q)_!F_m\to F_n$.  The quotient-fibre summation on
distributions is the dual of $\iota_{n,m}$ on the corresponding
\emph{costalk} face, with this geometric twist and shift retained.
It is not an ordinary-pullback transition on the stalks of $F_m$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The diagonal is finite and representable, so its shriek and star
images agree.  Adjunction for $\Delta_{\mathcal X_m}^*$ and
$\Delta_{\mathcal X_m,*}$ constructs \eqref{eq:root-diagonal-branch-beta}
from
\[
 q^*\Delta_{\mathcal X_n}^*\Delta_{\mathcal X_n,*}\Lambda_r
       \longrightarrow q^*\Lambda_r=\Lambda_r,
\]
using the actual diagonal counit.  No full faithfulness of the
stack diagonal is used.  On finite group representations this
counit is evaluation at the identity coset in the coinduced
permutation module.  Its adjoint is pullback of functions along
$G_m\to G_n$, giving the stated $\iota_{n,m}$.  The adjunction
unit/counit identities identify the maps for three levels.

On the product atlas at level $m$, the open diagonal has the
$|G_m|$ branches $z'_m=\zeta z_m$.  Its degree-zero product-open
link is therefore $\Lambda_r[G_m]$.  The attaching map from
$F_m$ is the identity on these branches.  For $(q\times q)^*F_n$
it is $\iota_{n,m}$: the label of a level-$n$ branch restricts to
all its level-$m$ lifts.  Consequently the square for $\beta$ is
\[
\begin{CD}
 \Lambda_r[G_n] @>{\iota_{n,m}}>> \Lambda_r[G_m]\\
 @V{\iota_{n,m}}VV @VV{1}V\\
 \Lambda_r[G_m] @>{1}>> \Lambda_r[G_m].
\end{CD}
\]
This calculation is on the actual branches, with their permutation
actions.  Higher link degrees and the connecting arrow are retained
in the actual adjunction map, rather than replaced by a splitting
of their cohomology.

Apply geometric Verdier duality to this whole map.  The identity
$D(q\times q)^*= (q\times q)^!D$, followed by shriek adjunction,
gives \eqref{eq:root-diagonal-branch-trace}.  Proper duality for
the diagonal and the smooth-branch dualizing coefficient give
$D F_n=\Delta_{\mathcal X_n,*}\Lambda_r(1)[2]$.
Dualizing a stalk map gives a costalk map.  On these finite free
coefficient faces the transpose of $\iota_{n,m}$ is
$[b]\mapsto[\bar b]$.  This proves the assertion about the
specified distribution transition and its type.  Dualizing the
composition identity for $\beta$ proves trace transitivity in the
same diagram, including its boundary mates.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A stalkwise distribution gluing is impossible]
\label{cor:root-diagonal-stalk-obstruction}
On this branch there is no morphism
$F_m\to(q\times q)^*F_n$ that is the identity on the open
diagonal and is quotient-fibre summation on the closed atlas
fibre.  More generally there is no such morphism with any map on
the closed fibre.  Thus a global completed unit cannot be built
by gluing the local regular distribution modules using ordinary
pullback and unchanged open unit maps.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Compatibility with the degree-zero attaching maps would require
$\iota_{n,m}c=1$ on $\Lambda_r[G_m]$, where
$c:\Lambda_r[G_m]\to\Lambda_r[G_n]$ is the putative closed
map.  Reduction modulo $\ell$ makes this impossible by dimension,
since $|G_n|<|G_m|$.  For the proposed quotient map specifically,
$\iota_{n,m}c$ sends a basis vector to the sum of all vectors in
its quotient fibre; it is not the identity, even before reduction.
This obstruction is already visible in the degree-zero face and
cannot be repaired by a higher homotopy of the same maps.
\end{proof}

The corollary does not rule out a completed root shriek interface:
\eqref{eq:root-diagonal-branch-trace} is its actual alternative
transition.  It does rule out identifying that exceptional
transition with an ordinary stalkwise inverse system.  In
particular the local pseudocompact module source does not itself
supply the global sheaf or costalk descent category.  On a whole
singular fibre, one must retain its actual dualizing complex in
this construction; the smooth-branch formula $(1)[2]$ cannot be
used as the dualizing complex of a nodal union.

\begin{lemma}[The singular-support dualizing correction is not a line]
\label{lem:root-nodal-dualizing-correction}
On an ordinary strictly local nodal curve $C$ with two smooth
branches, the stalk of $\omega_C=D_C\Lambda_r$ at the node $i$
has
\[
 H^{-2}(i^*\omega_C)=\Lambda_r(1)^{\oplus2},\qquad
 H^{-1}(i^*\omega_C)=\Lambda_r,
\]
and no other cohomology.  In particular $\omega_C$ is not an
invertible shifted coefficient line.  The same local test applies
on a reduced nodal atlas, with the actual stabilizer action retained.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For the punctured inclusion $j$, its two disjoint branches give
$H^0(i^*Rj_*\Lambda_r)=\Lambda_r^{\oplus2}$ and
$H^1(i^*Rj_*\Lambda_r)=\Lambda_r(-1)^{\oplus2}$.  The restriction
of the constant sheaf in degree zero is the diagonal map.
The localization fibre sequence
\[
 i^!\Lambda_r\longrightarrow\Lambda_r
                   \longrightarrow i^*Rj_*\Lambda_r
\]
therefore has costalk cohomology $\Lambda_r$ in degree one and
$\Lambda_r(-1)^{\oplus2}$ in degree two.  These modules are free
over $\Lambda_r$.  Point Verdier duality identifies
$i^*\omega_C=D_{\Lambda_r}i^!\Lambda_r$, yielding the stated
cohomology.  An invertible complex over a local coefficient ring
has one rank-one cohomology line after reduction to the residue
field, which this stalk does not.  This is a necessary local test
on an atlas as well; forgetting its stabilizer action cannot turn
an invertible equivariant line into the displayed complex.
\end{proof}

The obstruction excludes ordinary stalk/pullback distribution
completion, not every root realization.  The actual mate retains
$\Delta_*\omega_X$, and the preceding calculation prevents replacing
its coefficient by one invertible line on a nodal support.
Constructing a kernel calculus with compatible units, transitions
and convolution is a separate problem.  No such global root source
or fourth main theorem is asserted here.  The independent research
draft \emph{Exceptional and Completed Root Kernel Calculus: A
Single-Transition Problem} records that problem and the auxiliary
finite diagrams.  Its first test concerns one complete nodal support,
one actual transition and one crossing rectangle; the four-model
coherence problem depends on that test.
