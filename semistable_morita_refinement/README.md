# Morita Coefficients, Incidence, and Semistable Refinement

**A supported comparison for a subdivided annulus**

## Main content

This paper studies the explicit blow-up of a thickness-two nodal model. It
computes the affine charts, exceptional support, and tube subdivision, then
constructs supported Morita comparisons for geometric rational-unipotent and
finite logarithmic coefficients. The comparison retains generation,
recollement, attaching maps, Verdier partners, minimal diagonal kernels, and
the relevant coefficient actions. Fixed-thickness integer-cut models are
organized over a coarse support index, and a one-modification bridge connects
the construction with the companion local Kummer realization.

## Scope and limitations

The principal results are pairwise comparisons and a coherent diagram over a
fixed coarse support index. They do not establish an unrestricted coherent
support/correspondence profile, arbitrary model or base-change independence,
full relative-product convolution, a general base-inertia/logarithmic
comparison, analytic compatibility beyond the stated local interface, or
integral supported refinement. The full exceptional constructible category is
also shown not to be refinement invariant by a $K_0$ obstruction.
